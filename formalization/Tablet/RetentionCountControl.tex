\begin{definition}[Simultaneous retention count control]
For $G,L,R$, real $q,\xi$ and extended nonnegative $\eta$, say that a
sample $\omega$ has retention count control if all the following hold,
where $A(U,W)$ is the original ordered-edge set and $K$ is the count of
\noderef{IndependentRetentionCount}:
\[
|K_{A(\{v\},R)}-q|A(\{v\},R)||\leq\xi|R|\quad(v\in L),
\qquad
|K_{A(L,\{w\})}-q|A(L,\{w\})||\leq\xi|L|\quad(w\in R),
\]
and, for every $U\subseteq L,W\subseteq R$ with
$|U|>\eta|L|,|W|>\eta|R|$,
\[
|K_{A(U,W)}-q|A(U,W)||\leq\xi|U||W|.
\]
All counts and errors in these inequalities are real numbers. The size
thresholds are interpreted in the extended nonnegative reals.
\end{definition}
