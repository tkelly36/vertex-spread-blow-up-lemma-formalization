\begin{lemma}[Rectangle inequalities bound the cut expression]
Let $G$ be a simple graph and let $U,W$ be finite sets with
$|U|=|W|=n$. Let $k\in\mathbb N$ and suppose
$k\max\{0,|X|+|Y|-n\}\leq e_G(X,Y)$ for every $X\subseteq U$ and
$Y\subseteq W$. Then, for every $A\subseteq U$ and $B\subseteq W$,
\[
 kn\leq k|A|+e_G(U\setminus A,W\setminus B)+k|B|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $a=|A|$, $b=|B|$. The subset hypotheses give $a,b\leq n$ and
$|U\setminus A|=n-a$, $|W\setminus B|=n-b$. If $a+b\leq n$, applying
the rectangle inequality to these two complements gives
$e_G(U\setminus A,W\setminus B)\geq k(n-a-b)$. Adding $ka+kb$ yields
$kn$. If $a+b>n$, already $ka+kb=k(a+b)\geq kn$, and the edge count
is nonnegative. This also covers $n=0$ and $k=0$.
\end{proof}
