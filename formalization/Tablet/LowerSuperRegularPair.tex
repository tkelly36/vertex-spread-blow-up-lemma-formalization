\begin{definition}
Let $G$ be a simple graph on an arbitrary vertex type, let $V_1,V_2$ be arbitrary finite vertex subsets, and let $\varepsilon,d$ be arbitrary extended nonnegative reals.  The ordered pair $(V_1,V_2)$ is lower $(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular if it is lower $(\varepsilon,d)$-regular in the sense of \noderef{LowerRegularPair}, every $v\in V_1$ has at least $(d-\varepsilon)|V_2|$ neighbors in $V_2$, and every $w\in V_2$ has at least $(d-\varepsilon)|V_1|$ neighbors in $V_1$.  The paper uses this predicate for finite ambient graphs, disjoint cluster fibers, $\varepsilon>0$, and $0<d<1$.
\end{definition}
