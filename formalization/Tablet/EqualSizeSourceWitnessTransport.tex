\begin{lemma}[Equal-size pullback and contained transport]
In a finite disjoint-union ambient vertex set, let $U,K,V,Z$ have size
$m$, with $U\cap K=U\cap Z=K\cap Z=V\cap Z=\varnothing$.
Let $M,O,W,J,H$ be graphs and let $\tau:U\to V$ be a bijection such that
$uv\in M$ if and only if $v=\tau(u)$, for $u\in U,v\in V$.
Assume $W\subseteq O$, $J\subseteq H$, $\xi\le\eta$, $\omega\le e$,
and $W[V,Z]$ is $(\xi,p)$-super-regular.
There is a graph $F$ with $F[U,Z]$ $(\eta,p)$-super-regular and
$uz\in F\Longleftrightarrow\tau(u)z\in W$ on $U\times Z$.
For every bijection $\sigma:U\to K$, if the graph on subtype copies of
$U,Z$ with edges $uz\in F$ and $\sigma(u)z\in J$ is
$(\omega,ph)$-super-regular, then the graph
\[
 T_\sigma:\quad vz\in E(T_\sigma)\Longleftrightarrow
 v\in V,\ z\in Z,\ vz\in O,\quad
 \forall u\in U\ (uv\in M\Rightarrow\sigma(u)z\in H),
\]
symmetrized as a simple graph, contains a spanning
$(e,ph)$-super-regular graph on $V,Z$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Define $F$ to have just the edges $uz$ with $u\in U,z\in Z$ and
$\tau(u)z\in W$, and their reverses. Disjointness excludes loops and
ambiguity between the two orientations. The bijection $\tau$ preserves
every degree on the opposite side and every edge count in a tested
rectangle: the rectangle $A\times B$ corresponds to
$\tau(A)\times B$, with unchanged cardinalities. Thus the degree and
regularity inequalities in \noderef{SuperRegularPair} hold with error
$\xi$ and hence with error $\eta$. Error enlargement decreases the lower
bounds, increases the upper bounds, and only restricts the sets tested for
regularity.

For a given $\sigma$, define $W_{\rm new}$ to have just the edges
$\tau(u)z$ for which $uz\in F$ and $\sigma(u)z\in J$, and their
reverses. It is not defined to be $T_\sigma$. On these edges, $W\subseteq O$
gives the old constraint and $J\subseteq H$ gives the new constraint.
The source-matching equivalence says that $u$ is the only vertex of $U$
adjacent in $M$ to $\tau(u)$. Therefore all the new constraints hold and
$W_{\rm new}\subseteq T_\sigma$.

Apply \noderef{MatchingInducedUpdatedCandidacyTransport} with the
source bijection $\tau$, the graph $W_{\rm new}$ as its target graph,
and the complete bipartite graph on $U,K$ as its matching graph. The
bijection $\sigma$ is a perfect matching of that graph in the sense of
\noderef{PerfectMatching}. The target adjacency equivalence is precisely
the definition of $W_{\rm new}$, not an equality with $T_\sigma$.
The required part conditions are the two equivalences
$v\in V\Longleftrightarrow\exists u\in U\ v=\tau(u)$ and
$z\in Z\Longleftrightarrow\exists w\in Z\ z=w$.
Both follow from bijectivity and the identity on $Z$.
Transport gives error $\omega$ on the exact parts $V,Z$. Enlarge that
error to $e$ by the degree and rectangle inequalities just described.
This proves the required spanning contained witness.
\end{proof}
