\begin{definition}
Given a blow-up instance with candidacy system \noderef{CompatibleEmbedding} and a finite set $X\subseteq V(H)$, a partial compatible embedding of the induced subgraph $H[X]$ in $G$ is a map $f:V(H)\to V(G)$ whose restriction to $X$ is injective, which sends every edge of $H$ with both endpoints in $X$ to an edge of $G$, and such that $A(x,f(x))$ holds for every $x\in X$.  Values of $f$ outside $X$ are ignored.
\end{definition}
