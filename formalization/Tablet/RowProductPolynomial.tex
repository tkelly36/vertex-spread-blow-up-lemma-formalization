\begin{definition}
For a real $n\times n$ matrix $A$, its row-product polynomial is the
Mathlib multivariate polynomial
\[
 P_A(X)=\prod_{i\in\mathrm{Fin}(n)}\sum_{j\in\mathrm{Fin}(n)} A_{ij}X_j.
\]
An empty product is one. Permanents below are Mathlib's
$\operatorname{per}(A)=\sum_\sigma\prod_j A_{\sigma(j),j}$.
\end{definition}
