\begin{definition}[Logarithmic matching-count correction]
For real $d,\varepsilon$ define
\[
 L(d,\varepsilon)=\log{d+\varepsilon+3\sqrt\varepsilon\over d-2\varepsilon}
 -3\sqrt\varepsilon\log\min(d+\varepsilon+3\sqrt\varepsilon,1).
\]
The square root and logarithm are the total real functions: square root
of a negative number is zero, $\log0=0$, and the logarithm of a negative
number is the logarithm of its absolute value; division by zero is zero.
In applications $d>2\varepsilon>0$, so every
logarithm argument in this formula is positive. Thus $e^{L(d,\varepsilon)}$
is the correction factor for the loss from small rectangles and the
ratio of upper and lower matching-count densities.
\end{definition}
