\begin{definition}
Let $H,G$ be finite graphs and let $R$ be a graph on $[r]$.  A blow-up instance consists of cluster maps $c_H:V(H)\to[r]$ and $c_G:V(G)\to[r]$.  Writing $X_i=c_H^{-1}(i)$ and $V_i=c_G^{-1}(i)$, it is required that every $X_i$ is independent in $H$, that $|X_i|\leq|V_i|$ for every $i$, and that every edge $xy\in E(H)$ satisfies $c_H(x)c_H(y)\in E(R)$.  The fibers of the two cluster maps are, intrinsically, partitions of the respective vertex sets.
\end{definition}
