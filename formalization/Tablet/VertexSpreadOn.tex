\begin{definition}
Let $X,Y$ be arbitrary types, let $U\subseteq Y$, let $q$ be an extended nonnegative real, and let $\mu$ be a probability mass function on the functions $X\to Y$.  We say that $\mu$ is \emph{$q$-vertex-spread on $U$} if $q\leq1$ and, for every natural number $s$, every injective map $x:[s]\to X$, and every map $y:[s]\to Y$ whose image lies in $U$, one has
\[
 \mu\bigl(\operatorname{Cyl}(x,y)\bigr)\leq q^s.
\]
Here the cylinder event is the one in \noderef{CylinderEvent}.  The paper applies this definition when $X$ and $Y$ are finite sets and $q\in[0,1]$.
\end{definition}
