\begin{lemma}[Near-equal Four Graphs lemma]
For all $0<d_{12},d_{13},d_{23}\leq1$ there exists $\varepsilon'_0>0$ such that, for every $0<\varepsilon'\leq\varepsilon'_0$, there exists $\varepsilon_0>0$ such that, for every $0<\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon_0$, there exists $a_0>0$ such that, for every $0<a\leq a_0$, there exists $n_0$ with the following property.  Let $m\geq n_0$, let $|W_1|=|W_2|=m$, and let $|W_3|\in\{m-1,m,m+1\}$.  If $(F_{12},F_{13},F_{23})$ is a near-equal $(\varepsilon,d_{12},d_{13},d_{23})$-regular triplet in the sense of \noderef{NearEqualRegularTriplet}, then, for every witness that $F_{12}[W_1,W_2]$ has a perfect matching and a uniformly random such matching $\sigma$ in the sense of \noderef{UniformPerfectMatching},
\[
 \Pr\bigl[A_\sigma\text{ is }(\varepsilon',d_{13}d_{23})\text{-super-regular on }W_1,W_3\bigr]
 \geq1-(1-a)^m,
\]
where $A_\sigma$ is \noderef{MatchingInducedGraph}.  The constants and failure bound are uniform over all three permitted values of $|W_3|$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $q=d_{13}d_{23}$ and first choose $0<\varepsilon'_0\leq1$.
Fix $0<\varepsilon'\leq\varepsilon'_0$, and put $k=|W_3|$.
Use \noderef{NearEqualFourGraphsParameters} with $\eta=\varepsilon'$
to obtain positive finite $\delta,\zeta,e$, with
$\delta\leq\min(q,\varepsilon')$, $32\delta\leq(\varepsilon')^5$,
and all the degree, set-size, and polynomial windows stated there for
every $0\leq\varepsilon\leq e$.
Invoke \noderef{UniformMatchingSetImageConcentration}, always with matching
density $d_{12}$ and additive error $\zeta$, first with lower set-density
bounds $d_{13}/2,d_{23}/2$, and then with lower bounds
$(d_{13}/2)^2,(d_{23}/2)^2$.  Let $\theta_{\rm deg}>0$ and
$\theta_{\rm cod}>0$ be the two admissible input-error thresholds supplied
by that node.

Choose $\varepsilon_0=\min(e,\theta_{\rm deg},\theta_{\rm cod})>0$.
For every $0<\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon_0$, the parameter lemma gives
\[
 2\varepsilon<\min(d_{12},d_{13},d_{23}),\qquad
 \varepsilon\leq\min(\delta,\varepsilon'),
\]
as well as $d_{ij}/2\leq d_{ij}-\varepsilon$ and
$(d_{ij}/2)^2\leq(d_{ij}-2\varepsilon)^2$ for $ij=13,23$.
Its two sharp degree inequalities are
\[
 q-\delta\leq(d_{13}-\varepsilon)(d_{23}-\varepsilon)-\zeta,
 \qquad
 (d_{13}+\varepsilon)(d_{23}+\varepsilon)+\zeta\leq q+\delta.
\]
Its polynomial window is
\[
 (d_{13}+2\varepsilon)^2(d_{23}+2\varepsilon)^2+\zeta
 \leq(q+\delta)^2.
\tag{1}
\]

Fix any $0<\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon_0$.  The two concentration
invocations now apply.  Take the larger of their size thresholds and the
larger of their exponential constants, calling the latter $c<1$; increasing
a constant below $1$ only weakens the asserted upper bound.  Thus one $c$
and one threshold work for every vertex-neighborhood and good-codegree set
used below.  Denote this common size threshold by $N_*$.  The relation
$|k-m|\leq1$ gives explicitly
\[
 m-1\leq k\leq m+1,
 \qquad 1-m^{-1}\leq k/m\leq1+m^{-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
We will use the upper inequality in the union bounds.  The degree windows
below retain their actual opposite-part cardinalities, and the near-equal
regularity criterion itself accepts $k\in\{m-1,m,m+1\}$, so no unrecorded
replacement of $k$ by $m$ is needed.  The perfect-matching witness in the
statement makes the uniform distribution well-defined.

The adjacency and cardinality identifications on the copied graph
are supplied by \noderef{MatchingInducedGraphIdentities}.
For $v\in W_1$, the matching edge $v\sigma(v)$ lies in $F_{12}$.
The last clause of \noderef{NearEqualRegularTriplet}, normalized by the actual $k$, gives deterministically
\[
 \deg_{A_\sigma}(v)=(q\pm\varepsilon)k.
\tag{3}
\]
For $w\in W_3$, put $S=N_{F_{13}}(w)\cap W_1$ and $T=N_{F_{23}}(w)\cap W_2$.  Both sets are normalized by $m$, because their opposite parts are $W_1,W_2$, and
\[
 \deg_{A_\sigma}(w)=|\sigma(S)\cap T|.
\]
Super-regularity gives $|S|=(d_{13}\pm\varepsilon)m$ and
$|T|=(d_{23}\pm\varepsilon)m$. The lower endpoints are at least
$(d_{13}/2)m,(d_{23}/2)m$, respectively, so the first concentration
invocation applies. Its center $|S||T|/m$ lies between
$(d_{13}-\varepsilon)(d_{23}-\varepsilon)m$ and
$(d_{13}+\varepsilon)(d_{23}+\varepsilon)m$.
The two sharp degree inequalities above put the concentration interval
inside $[(q-\delta)m,(q+\delta)m]$.
\noderef{MatchingInducedDegreeConcentration} makes precisely these
estimates simultaneous, with failure at most $kc^m$.
In particular every such $w$ has at least $(q-\delta)m$ neighbors,
exactly the criterion's lower-degree condition. Also $\delta\leq\varepsilon'$
puts these degrees in the final super-regularity window.

On the $W_1$ side, (3) is already normalized by the actual opposite-part
size $k$, and $\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon'$ puts it in the
required $(q\pm\varepsilon')k$ window.  Thus no replacement of $k$ by $m$
is made in either final degree condition.

For regularity, apply \noderef{NearEqualRegularPairGoodCodegrees} to $F_{13}[W_1,W_3]$ and $F_{23}[W_2,W_3]$.  At most $6\varepsilon k^2$ ordered pairs of $W_3^2$ are bad for one of the two input graphs.  For any remaining $(w,w')$, take the two common-neighborhood sets $S\subseteq W_1$ and $T\subseteq W_2$.  Their sizes are at most $(d_{13}+2\varepsilon)^2m$ and $(d_{23}+2\varepsilon)^2m$.  Set-image concentration and (1) imply
\[
 |N_{A_\sigma}(w)\cap N_{A_\sigma}(w')|
 =|\sigma(S)\cap T|\leq(q+\delta)^2m
\]
except with probability $c^m$.  Here the good-codegree lower bounds are at
least $(d_{13}-2\varepsilon)^2m\geq(d_{13}/2)^2m$ and
$(d_{23}-2\varepsilon)^2m\geq(d_{23}/2)^2m$, so the second concentration
invocation really applies; its conclusion differs from $|S||T|/m$ by at
most $\zeta m$, while (1) gives the displayed upper bound.
Applying \noderef{MatchingInducedCodegreeConcentration} to these two
exceptional-set bounds, the squared lower set-size bounds, (1), and the
second concentration invocation gives the following simultaneous event.

There are at most $k^2\leq(m+1)^2$ good ordered pairs, with diagonal pairs
retained. A union bound makes their concentration estimates simultaneous. On that event, every pair
which is good in both input graphs satisfies the displayed codegree bound,
and the retained vertex estimate gives the criterion's $(q-\delta)m$ lower
degree bound.  Hence only the at most $6\varepsilon k^2\leq6\delta k^2$
input-bad ordered pairs are bad for the output.  We have
$0\leq\delta\leq q\leq1$, $\varepsilon'\leq1$, $1\leq\delta m$, and
$32\delta\leq(\varepsilon')^5$ by the choices above. Therefore the
deterministic adapter \noderef{MatchingInducedSuperRegularCertificate},
which applies \noderef{NearEqualDegreeCodegreeRegularityCriterion} with
exactly these sizes and exceptional-pair allowance, combines this regularity
estimate with the two degree sides to prove $(\varepsilon',q)$-super-regularity
in the sense of \noderef{SuperRegularPair}.

The first union bound has $k$ events and the second has $k^2$ events, so the
total failure probability is explicitly
\[
 kc^m+k^2c^m\leq\bigl((m+1)+(m+1)^2\bigr)c^m.
\]
Use \noderef{FourGraphsPolynomialLossAbsorption} to select $a_0>0$
after $\varepsilon$ and the common $c$ have been fixed. For every
$0<a\leq a_0$ it supplies a threshold above which the last display is
at most $(1-a)^m$, including when $c=0$. Take $n_0$ at least this
threshold and $N_*$, and at least $2$ and $1/\delta$ (rounded up).
Then $1\leq\delta m$ for every $m\geq n_0$, as needed above.
Thus the success probability is at least $1-(1-a)^m$.
All thresholds and both union bounds used only
$k\leq m+1$ and $|k-m|\leq1$, so they are uniform for
$k=m-1,m,m+1$, as required.
\end{proof}
