\begin{lemma}[Intersection tails for product-permutation blocks]
Let $I$ be finite and let $F_j$ be finite sets. Fix $i\in I$ with
$|F_i|=n>0$, subsets $S,T\subseteq F_i$, and a real number $a>0$.
For $\Omega=\prod_{j\in I}\operatorname{Sym}(F_j)$, one has
\[
\frac{|\{\omega\in\Omega:
 \bigl||\omega_i(S)\cap T|-|S||T|/n\bigr|>an\}|}{|\Omega|}
\leq 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{a^2n}{2(1+a/3)}\right).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{ProductPermutationSubsetLaw}, the fraction on the left is
the fraction of $|S|$-subsets $U$ of $F_i$ for which
$\bigl||U\cap T|-|S||T|/n\bigr|>an$.
The integer $|U\cap T|$ lies between $0$ and $n$, since
$U\cap T\subseteq T\subseteq F_i$. Partition this event according
to that integer $x$. By \noderef{UniformSubsetIntersectionLaw},
the fraction of all $|S|$-subsets giving the value $x$ is
$H(n,|S|,|T|,x)$. The required fraction is therefore exactly the sum
of these masses over $0\leq x\leq n$ with
$|x-|S||T|/n|>an$. Both subset sizes are at most $n$.
Applying \noderef{HypergeometricTwoTail} with these sizes and the
positive parameter $a$ bounds that sum by the displayed quantity.
All events use the original product-permutation space; no independence
of intersection counts is asserted or needed.
\end{proof}
