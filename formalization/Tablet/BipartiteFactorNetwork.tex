\begin{definition}[Network for a bipartite regular factor]
Given a simple graph $G$, finite sets $U,W$ of vertices, and $k\in\mathbb N$,
the factor network, in the sense of \noderef{FiniteCapacityNetwork}, has
vertex type $(\{s\}\sqcup U)\sqcup(W\sqcup\{t\})$, implemented as
$\operatorname{Option}(U)\sqcup\operatorname{Option}(W)$.
The source is the left None and the sink the right None; the left and
right Some vertices are exact subtype copies of $U$ and $W$, respectively.
They and the terminals are pairwise disjoint, even if the ambient sets
overlap or are empty. Its capacities are $c(s,u)=k$, $c(w,t)=k$, and
$c(u,w)=1$ if $G$ has the edge $uw$, with every other capacity zero.
Each ordered pair carries exactly one capacity entry, not multiple arcs.
\end{definition}
