\begin{lemma}[Degree counting inside a rectangle]
Let $U,W$ be finite sets, $P$ a relation between their ambient elements,
and $k\in\mathbb N$. Suppose every $u\in U$ is related to at least $k$
elements of $W$, and every $w\in W$ is related from at most $k$ elements
of $U$. Then for all $A\subseteq U$, $B\subseteq W$,
\[
 k\max\{0,|A|+|B|-|W|\}\leq
 |\{(u,w)\in A\times B:P(u,w)\}|.
\]
No equality of part sizes, symmetry of $P$, or disjointness is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $e(X,Y)$ for the ordered-pair count in the statement. By
\noderef{FilteredProductRowCount},
$e(A,W)=\sum_{u\in A}|\{w\in W:P(u,w)\}|\geq k|A|$.
Counting the pairs with right endpoint in $W\setminus B$ by columns gives
$e(A,W\setminus B)=\sum_{w\in W\setminus B}|\{u\in A:P(u,w)\}|$:
this is the same finite double sum with its summation order exchanged.
Since $A\subseteq U$, each column count is at most its count over $U$,
hence at most $k$. Thus $e(A,W\setminus B)\leq k(|W|-|B|)$.
Partitioning right endpoints into $B$ and $W\setminus B$ gives
$e(A,W)=e(A,B)+e(A,W\setminus B)$. It follows that
$k|A|\leq e(A,B)+k(|W|-|B|)$.
If $|A|+|B|\geq |W|$, then
$|A|=(|A|+|B|-|W|)+(|W|-|B|)$, so cancelling
$k(|W|-|B|)$ yields the desired inequality. Otherwise its left side
is zero and nonnegativity of the pair count proves it. All these counts
include empty sets and $k=0$.
\end{proof}
