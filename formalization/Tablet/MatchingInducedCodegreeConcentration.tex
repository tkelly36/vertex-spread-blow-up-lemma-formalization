\begin{lemma}[Simultaneous good-pair codegrees from set-image concentration]
Let $G,F,H$ be graphs on a finite vertex set, $U,W,Z$ finite parts with
$U,W$ disjoint and $|U|=|W|=m>0$, and let $\mu$ be a probability mass
function on perfect matchings $f:U\to W$ of $G$ in the sense of
\noderef{PerfectMatching}. Put $k=|Z|$. All scalar parameters are
extended nonnegative reals. Suppose
\[
 (x/2)^2\leq(x-2\varepsilon)^2,\quad
 (y/2)^2\leq(y-2\varepsilon)^2,\quad
 (x+2\varepsilon)^2(y+2\varepsilon)^2+\zeta\leq(xy+\delta)^2.
\]
Suppose at most $3\varepsilon k^2$ ordered pairs $(z,z')\in Z^2$
have their common-neighborhood size in $U$ for $F$ outside
$[(x-2\varepsilon)^2m,(x+2\varepsilon)^2m]$, and at most
$3\varepsilon k^2$ have their common-neighborhood size in $W$ for $H$
outside $[(y-2\varepsilon)^2m,(y+2\varepsilon)^2m]$.
Suppose also that for every $S\subseteq U,T\subseteq W$ of sizes at least
$(x/2)^2m,(y/2)^2m$, respectively, the event
\[
 |S||T|/m-\zeta m\leq |\{u\in S:f(u)\in T\}|
 \leq |S||T|/m+\zeta m
\]
has probability at least $1-c^m$.
Then with probability at least $1-k^2c^m$, at most
$6\varepsilon k^2$ ordered pairs of the copied right part of
$A_f=\noderef{MatchingInducedGraph}(F,H,U,W,Z,f)$ have more than
$(xy+\delta)^2m$ common neighbors in its copied left part.
Diagonal ordered pairs are included in every count. Subtractions are
truncated at zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $B_F,B_H\subseteq Z^2$ be the two exceptional sets specified in the
statement, and put $B=B_F\cup B_H$. Then
$|B|\leq |B_F|+|B_H|\leq6\varepsilon k^2$.
For each $(z,z')\notin B$, set
\[
 S=\{u\in U:F(u,z)\land F(u,z')\},\quad
 T=\{w\in W:H(w,z)\land H(w,z')\}.
\]
Not being in either exceptional set gives both endpoints of each input
codegree window, in particular
\[
 |S|\geq(x-2\varepsilon)^2m\geq(x/2)^2m,\quad
 |T|\geq(y-2\varepsilon)^2m\geq(y/2)^2m.
\]
Thus the assumed concentration estimate applies to these very sets.
The codegree identity of \noderef{MatchingInducedGraphIdentities} gives
the explicit copied-graph equality
\[
 |N_{A_f}(\operatorname{inr}z)\cap N_{A_f}(\operatorname{inr}z')\cap L|
 =|\{u\in U:(F(u,z)\land F(u,z'))\land
                         (H(f(u),z)\land H(f(u),z'))\}|
 =|\{u\in S:f(u)\in T\}|.
\]
On concentration success its upper bound is at most
\[
 |S||T|/m+\zeta m
 \leq\bigl((x+2\varepsilon)^2(y+2\varepsilon)^2+\zeta\bigr)m
 \leq(xy+\delta)^2m.
\]
The cancellation uses $m>0$; if an upper endpoint is infinite the
inequality is still valid in extended arithmetic.

There are at most $k^2$ good ordered pairs. For any event of probability
at least $1-c^m$, its failure has probability at most $c^m$: this is
automatic if $c^m\geq1$, and otherwise follows by taking complements
in a probability space. Finite subadditivity therefore bounds the
probability of any of these good-pair concentration failures by
$k^2c^m$. Outside their union, each output pair whose codegree exceeds
the target must belong to $B$. The bijection
$(z,z')\mapsto(\operatorname{inr}z,\operatorname{inr}z')$ preserves the
number of ordered pairs, so at most $|B|\leq6\varepsilon k^2$ output
pairs offend. Taking complements proves the conclusion. No step removes
diagonal pairs.
\end{proof}
