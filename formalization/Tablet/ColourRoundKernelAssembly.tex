\begin{lemma}[Assembly of locally certified extension laws]
Suppose $\mathcal I(t,S;X,\mu)$ of \noderef{ColourRoundInvariant}
holds and $i\in c^{-1}(t)\setminus S$. Put $Y=X_i$ and let $X'$ be
the source domain for the earlier colours and $S\cup\{i\}$.
For every $f\in\operatorname{supp}(\mu)$ suppose there is a law
$\nu_f$ on compatible partial embeddings of $Y$ for $A_f$, whose
restriction to $Y$ is $C/n$-vertex-spread. Suppose that for every
supported $g$ and every compatible lift $h$ on $X'$ agreeing with $f$
on $X$ and $g$ on $Y$, each unembedded neighbour $j$ of $i$ has a
contained spanning $(\varepsilon_{t+1},p_{e_j+1})$-super-regular
witness in its true updated candidacy graph at $h$, on the exact
unembedded source and unused host parts. Here
$e_j=|N_R(j)\cap(\{k:c(k)<t\}\cup S)|$.
Then there is a total kernel $\kappa$ from compatible embeddings on
$X$ to laws on those on $X'$, agreeing with each supported input on
$X$ and $C/n$-vertex-spread on $Y$, such that precisely
$\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{bind}}\kappa$ satisfies
$\mathcal I(t,S\cup\{i\};X',\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{bind}}\kappa)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The domain description in \noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} gives
$X'=X\cup Y$ and $X\cap Y=\varnothing$: a vertex in $Y$ has colour
$t$ and its cluster $i$ is not in $S$. For each supported $f$, choose
$\nu_f$ as in the hypothesis. For every local compatible embedding
$g$, apply \noderef{ExtendUpdatedPartialEmbedding} on these disjoint
domains to choose a compatible lift $e_f(g)$ on $X'$. It agrees with
$f$ on $X$ and $g$ on $Y$. Set $K_f=(e_f)_*\nu_f$.
Every supported member of $K_f$ is the image of a supported $g$;
therefore it agrees with $f$ on $X$ and has all the asserted neighbour
witnesses. Its restriction law on $Y$ equals the restriction law of
$\nu_f$, so it is $C/n$-vertex-spread.

The support of a probability mass function is nonempty. Choose a
supported $f_0$, and set $\kappa(f)=K_f$ on the support of $\mu$ and
$\kappa(f)=K_{f_0}$ elsewhere. This is a total kernel with the asserted
support and conditional-spread properties. No new conditioning is
performed. Inputs outside the old support have mass zero and contribute
nothing to the defining bind law.

We check every invariant clause for that law. Its round, degree and
square-colouring clauses are inherited. Every vertex of $S\cup\{i\}$
has colour $t$ by the old clause and the hypothesis on $i$; the domain
clause is the definition of $X'$. Any supported output $h$ of the bind
has some $f\in\operatorname{supp}(\mu)$ with
$h\in\operatorname{supp}(\kappa(f))$.
Fix a still unembedded $j$. By
\noderef{ColourRoundUntouchedGeometry}, its touched-neighbour set in
$S\cup\{i\}$ has size at most one. If $ij\in E(R)$, that set contains
$i$, its exposure is exactly $e_j+1\le D$, and its witness error is
$\varepsilon_{t+1}$. The local witness hypothesis, applied to the
chosen supported $g$ and its lift $h$, supplies precisely this witness
on the actual parts. If $ij\notin E(R)$, both its exposure and its
touched-neighbour set are unchanged. Apply
\noderef{UpdatedCandidacyNonneighborExtension}: cluster $j$ is
disjoint from $X'$, $j\ne i$, the new domain is $X\cup Y$, and the
lift agrees on $X$. It gives equality of the old and new true candidacy
sets and equivalence of the unused source and host part conditions.
Thus the old contained witness is still a witness, with exactly its old
error. This treats both previously touched clusters, whose error is
$\varepsilon_{t+1}$, and completely untouched clusters, whose error
is $\varepsilon_t$; neither is asserted to have a smaller error.

We verify joint spread directly for this same bind law. Write
$\alpha=C/n$, $o(f)=f|_X$, $v(h)=h|_Y$, and
$w(h)=h|_X\sqcup h|_Y$, a function on $X\sqcup Y$. For every
$f\in\operatorname{supp}(\mu)$ and
$h\in\operatorname{supp}(\kappa(f))$, the agreement on $X$ gives
$w(h)=o(f)\sqcup v(h)$. No claim is made that the whole record $f$
is determined by $o(f)$.

Fix a cylinder $\operatorname{Cyl}(x,y)$ of
\noderef{CylinderEvent}, where $x:[s]\to X\sqcup Y$ is injective
and $y:[s]\to V(G)$ is arbitrary. Partition its index set as
$L=\{\ell:x(\ell)\in X\}$ and
$T=\{\ell:x(\ell)\in Y\}$, with membership understood in the
respective summands. Put $a=|L|$ and $b=|T|$; then $a+b=s$.
For $\ell\in L$ write $x(\ell)=\operatorname{inl}(x_L(\ell))$,
and for $\ell\in T$ write
$x(\ell)=\operatorname{inr}(x_T(\ell))$.
Both $x_L$ and $x_T$ are injective, since $x$ is injective.
Let
\[
 E_X=\{u:X\to V(G):u(x_L(\ell))=y(\ell)\text{ for all }\ell\in L\},
 \qquad
 E_Y=\{v:Y\to V(G):v(x_T(\ell))=y(\ell)\text{ for all }\ell\in T\}.
\]
Reindexing $L$ and $T$ by $[a]$ and $[b]$ makes these permitted
cylinders. The old spread clause of
\noderef{ColourRoundInvariant}, interpreted by
\noderef{VertexSpreadOn}, gives
$(o_*\mu)(E_X)\le\alpha^a$. The conditional spread already proved
above gives $(v_*\kappa(f))(E_Y)\le\alpha^b$ for every supported
$f$. These statements also cover $a=0$ or $b=0$.

For a supported input-output pair, the identity for $w(h)$ implies
\[
 w(h)\in\operatorname{Cyl}(x,y)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad o(f)\in E_X\text{ and }v(h)\in E_Y.
\]
In the sum defining the conditional event probability, every
$h\notin\operatorname{supp}(\kappa(f))$ has weight zero. Therefore,
for every supported $f$, this equivalence gives the exact identity
\[
 (w_*\kappa(f))(\operatorname{Cyl}(x,y))
 =\boldsymbol1_{E_X}(o(f))\,(v_*\kappa(f))(E_Y)
 \le\boldsymbol1_{E_X}(o(f))\,\alpha^b.
\]
Expand bind and pushforward using their probability mass sums.
Inputs $f$ outside $\operatorname{supp}(\mu)$ again contribute zero,
regardless of the filler kernel. Summing the displayed inequality over
the full input records, not over their restrictions, yields
\begin{align*}
 (w_*(\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{bind}}\kappa))(\operatorname{Cyl}(x,y))
 &=\sum_f\mu(f)\,(w_*\kappa(f))(\operatorname{Cyl}(x,y))\\
 &\le\sum_f\mu(f)\,\boldsymbol1_{E_X}(o(f))\,\alpha^b\\
 &=(o_*\mu)(E_X)\,\alpha^b
 \le\alpha^a\alpha^b=\alpha^s.
\end{align*}
All sums and products are nonnegative extended-real ones, so this
calculation uses no division or positive-event assumption. Together
with $\alpha\le1$ from the old spread clause, it proves the full
joint spread property on $X\sqcup Y$ in
\noderef{VertexSpreadOn}.

Finally send each vertex of $X'=X\cup Y$ to its summand in
$X\sqcup Y$. This map is injective: forgetting the summand recovers
the original vertex. Apply \noderef{VertexSpreadRestriction} to this
injection with target set $U=V(G)$. Composing $w(h)$ with the injection
gives exactly $h|_{X'}$, so functoriality of pushforward identifies the
resulting law with the restriction of
$\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{bind}}\kappa$ to $X'$.
All the clauses of the required invariant now hold.
\end{proof}
