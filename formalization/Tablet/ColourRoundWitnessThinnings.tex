\begin{lemma}[Contained thinnings of the full untouched witnesses]
Let $B,A,c,\theta,p,\varepsilon,\rho,\zeta,\omega,\xi,D,d,C,a,n,t$
be an instance, candidacy system, colouring, sequences and scalars, with a
\noderef{ColourRoundStepParameters} record at these parameters.
Assume every source cluster has size $n$ or $n+1$ and equals its host
cluster in size, the original host pairs are
$(\varepsilon_0,d)$-super-regular, and
\noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} holds for $(t,S,X,\mu)$.
Fix $i$ with $c(i)=t$, $i\notin S$, and $f\in\operatorname{supp}\mu$.
Let $L_j=\mathrm{inl}(X_j)$, $Q_j=\mathrm{inr}(V_j)$ be the full copied
clusters, let
$\mathcal J=\{j:ij\in E(R),j\notin S,c(j)\ge t\}$, and put
$e_j=|N_R(j)\cap(\{k:c(k)<t\}\cup S)|$.
On the source/host sum let $A_f^j$ be the symmetrization of
$\mathrm{inl}(x)\mathrm{inr}(v)$ for
$(x,v)\in\noderef{UpdatedCandidacyCluster}$ at $(B,A,X,f,j)$,
and let $H_0$ have exactly the original host edges on host copies and
no edges incident to source copies.
Then $e_i\le D$ and $e_j<D$ for every $j\in\mathcal J$.
There are graphs $W_{12}$ and families $O_j,W_j,J_j$ on the ambient sum
with $W_{12}\subseteq A_f^i$ and
$(W_{12};L_i,Q_i)$ $(\xi_t,\rho_{e_i})$-super-regular, and, for each
$j\in\mathcal J$,
\[
 O_j\subseteq A_f^j,\quad
 (O_j;L_j,Q_j)\text{ is }(\varepsilon_t,p_{e_j})\text{-super-regular},
 \quad W_j\subseteq O_j,\quad
 (W_j;L_j,Q_j)\text{ is }(\xi_t,\rho_{e_j})\text{-super-regular},
\]
\[
 J_j\subseteq H_0,\qquad
 (J_j;Q_i,Q_j)\text{ is }(\xi_t,d/2)\text{-super-regular}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unpack \noderef{ColourRoundInvariant}. Its degree, square-colouring and
touched-colour clauses meet all hypotheses of
\noderef{ColourRoundUntouchedGeometry} at $i$. Thus the touched-neighbour
sets of $i$ and every $j\in\mathcal J$ are empty, and $e_j<D$ for each
such $j$. The invariant separately gives $e_i\le D$. No comparison of
$e_i$ and $e_j$ is made.

For any $j\notin S$ with $c(j)\ge t$, the exact domain clause says that
a vertex $x\in X_j$ cannot belong to $X$: membership would say
$c(j)<t$ or $j\in S$, both impossible. If a vertex $v\in V_j$ were
$f(x)$ for $x\in X$, compatibility in
\noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding} and the cluster-respecting clause
of \noderef{CandidacySystem} would give
$B.\mathrm{sourceCluster}(x)=B.\mathrm{hostCluster}(v)=j$.
This contradicts the preceding exclusion. Hence $V_j\cap f(X)$ is empty.
The two finite parts in the witness clause of
\noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} are therefore exactly $L_j$ and $Q_j$,
not merely subsets: each copy in the full cluster satisfies the unused
condition, and every vertex in a witness part is by its definition a
copy in that cluster. Since the touched-neighbour set is empty, its
conditional witness error is $\varepsilon_t$. Select its graph $O_j$,
with containment in $A_f^j$ as defined by
\noderef{UpdatedCandidacyCluster}. The same argument gives $O_i$.

The source and host injections into the sum preserve cardinalities and
have disjoint images. Thus $|L_j|=|Q_j|\in\{n,n+1\}$, so both
near-equal inequalities hold. The witness threshold in
\noderef{ColourRoundStepParameters} at $(t,e_j)$ is at most $n$ and
hence at most both part sizes. Its round bound is $t<q$; the invariant
provides the required exposure bound separately for each call.
Apply the witness-thinning guarantee of
\noderef{InductionApplicationCertificate} through
\noderef{FiniteSuperRegularThinning}. This adapter uses an equivalence
with a lifted finite ordinal in the guarantee's universe, preserves
both exact parts and sizes, and pulls back containment. It supplies
$W_j\subseteq O_j$ with parameters $(\xi_t,\rho_{e_j})$, and supplies
$W_{12}\subseteq O_i\subseteq A_f^i$ with parameters
$(\xi_t,\rho_{e_i})$.

For each $j\in\mathcal J$, the host clusters $V_i,V_j$ are disjoint:
the reduced edge implies $i\ne j$, while a common vertex would have
both cluster labels. Their sizes belong to $\{n,n+1\}$, giving both
near-equal inequalities. The host threshold from
\noderef{ColourRoundStepParameters} is at most $n$, hence at most
both sizes. The input super-regularity is the $ij$ clause of
\noderef{InstanceSuperRegular}. Apply the host-thinning guarantee of
\noderef{InductionApplicationCertificate}, again through
\noderef{FiniteSuperRegularThinning}, obtaining a graph $T_j\subseteq G$
with $(\xi_t,d/2)$ super-regularity on $V_i,V_j$.
Let $J_j$ have precisely the edges
$\mathrm{inr}(v)\mathrm{inr}(w)$ for $vw\in E(T_j)$, symmetrized
on the ambient sum. Its containment in $H_0$ follows edgewise from
$T_j\subseteq G$, in either orientation. For host vertices its
adjacency is equivalent to that of $T_j$: the forward implication
uses symmetry in the reversed orientation, and the backward implication
uses looplessness to satisfy the unequal-endpoint condition of the
symmetrization. Apply \noderef{SuperRegularPairEmbedding} with the
injective host map $\mathrm{inr}$. Its exact neighbourhood and rectangle
count identities give the desired super-regularity on $Q_i,Q_j$.
Choose these finitely many graphs together to obtain all families.
\end{proof}
