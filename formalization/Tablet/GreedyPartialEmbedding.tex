\begin{definition}[Greedy partial-embedding state]
A greedy state on $S$ is a partial compatible embedding as in
\noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding}, represented by a total map $f$,
with the additional invariant
\[
 d(d-\varepsilon)_+^{|S\cap N_H(x)|}n\leq |D_S^f(x)|
                  \qquad\text{for every }x\notin S.
\]
The sets $D_S^f(x)$ are those of \noderef{RawCandidates}; occupied
images are not removed in this invariant. The exponent is the actual
number of embedded neighbors, and subtraction is truncated subtraction
of extended nonnegative reals. Values of the total map outside $S$
are not otherwise constrained.
\end{definition}
