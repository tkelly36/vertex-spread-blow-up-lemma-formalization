\begin{lemma}[Transport of a restricted partial law]
Let $B,A$ be a finite blow-up instance and candidacy system, let $X\subseteq S$
be source subsets, and let $B_S,A_S$ be an instance on the literal subtype
$S$ with induced source graph, unchanged host and reduced graphs, and
$A_S(x,v)\iff A(x,v)$. Let $T\subseteq S$ consist exactly of the subtype
vertices whose ambient value belongs to $X$, and fix a function
$g:V(H)\to V(G)$. Suppose a law $\nu$ on partial compatible embeddings
of $T$ has $q$-spread total-map law on $S$ and, for every sampled map,
at least $\rho$ updated candidates at every vertex of $S\setminus T$.
Then there is a law $\mu$ on ambient partial compatible embeddings of $X$
whose restrictions to $X$ are $q$-spread. Every $f$ with $\mu(f)\ne0$ has
at least $\rho$ ambient updated candidates at every $x\in S\setminus X$.
All host values are allowed in both spread assertions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a sampled restricted map $t$, define $F_t(x)=t(\langle x,x\in S\rangle)$
when $x\in X$, and $F_t(x)=g(x)$ otherwise. Injectivity on $X$ follows from
injectivity on $T$ and injectivity of the subtype inclusion. The induced
source graph has exactly the original edges between its vertices, so
edge preservation on $X$ follows from edge preservation on $T$.
The assumed candidacy equivalence transfers compatibility on $X$.
These are all fields of \noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding}.
The two images are exactly equal, $F_t(X)=t(T)$: send $x\in X$ to its
subtype lift for one inclusion, and send a vertex of $T$ to its ambient
value for the other.

For $x\in S$, expand \noderef{UpdatedCandidacy}. The exclusion $x\notin X$
is equivalent to its lift not lying in $T$. The unused-host exclusion is
identical by the image equality. Original candidacy transfers by assumption.
For every embedded neighbor $y\in X$, its lift belongs to $T$, induced
adjacency is original adjacency, and $F_t(y)=t(y)$. Conversely every
$y\in T$ arises this way. Thus both directions of the updated-candidacy
equivalence hold, and the two candidate sets are literally equal, with
equal cardinalities, including exactly the same removed image.

Push $\nu$ forward by $t\mapsto F_t$ to define $\mu$. The law on restrictions
to $X$ is the restriction of the original law on $S$ along the injection
$X\hookrightarrow S$: equality holds pointwise on all coordinates of $X$,
and the two iterated PMF pushforwards therefore agree.
Apply \noderef{VertexSpreadRestriction} to this injection. This proves
spread on $X$; it asserts no spread for the fixed values outside $X$.
Finally the support of a PMF pushforward is the image of its support.
Every supported $F$ therefore has a sampled preimage $t$; transfer its
reserve by the candidate-set equality just proved. This also covers
noninjective pushforwards: no selection of a unique preimage is needed.
\end{proof}
