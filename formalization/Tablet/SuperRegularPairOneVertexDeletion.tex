\begin{lemma}[Deleting at most one vertex from each part]
Let $G$ be a simple graph with finite parts $L,R$ and subsets
$A\subseteq L,B\subseteq R$. Let $m\geq1$ be an integer with
$|A|,|B|\geq m$, $|L|\leq|A|+1$, and $|R|\leq|B|+1$.
For real parameters suppose $0\leq\tau$, $0<\eta\leq1$,
$0\leq c\leq1$, $4\tau\leq\eta$, and $\eta m\geq4$.
If $(L,R)$ is $(\tau,c)$-super-regular, then $(A,B)$ is
$(\eta,c)$-super-regular. All parameters in the pair predicates
are interpreted as extended nonnegative reals, with truncated lower endpoints.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Since $|A|,|B|\geq1$, we have $|L|\leq2|A|$ and
$|R|\leq2|B|$. Thus $\tau|L|\leq\eta|A|$ and
$\tau|R|\leq\eta|B|$. Every strict regularity test for $A,B$
is therefore a test for $L,R$. Its ordered-edge count and density
are the same, and the interval with error $\tau$ is contained in
the interval with error $\eta$. This gives \noderef{RegularPair}.

For either degree orientation, denote the original opposite part by
$S$, its retained subset by $T$, and the old and retained neighbor
counts by $a,b$. Since $|S\setminus T|\leq1$, we have
$b\leq a\leq b+1$. Put $k=|T|$ and $s=|S|$; then
$k\leq s\leq k+1$, $\eta k\geq4$, and $\tau k\leq\eta k/4$.
The degree bounds in \noderef{SuperRegularPair} give
$(c-\tau)s\leq a\leq(c+\tau)s$ as real inequalities:
if the first endpoint is negative, the first inequality is automatic.
If $c\geq\eta$, then $c-\tau\geq0$ and
\[
 b\geq(c-\tau)s-1\geq(c-\tau)k-1\geq(c-\eta)k,
\]
because $(\eta-\tau)k\geq3\eta k/4\geq3$.
If $c<\eta$, the required truncated lower bound is zero.
For the upper bound, $\tau\leq1$ and $c\leq1$ give
\[
 b\leq(c+\tau)(k+1)\leq(c+\tau)k+2\leq(c+\eta)k.
\]
Apply this argument at every vertex of $A$ with $S=R,T=B$, and
at every vertex of $B$ with $S=L,T=A$. These are both required
degree intervals, completing the three clauses of super-regularity.
\end{proof}
