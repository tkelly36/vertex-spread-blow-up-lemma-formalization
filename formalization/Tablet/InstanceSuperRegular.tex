\begin{definition}
A blow-up instance \noderef{BlowUpInstance} is host-$(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular if, for every edge $ij$ of the reduced graph $R$, the host clusters $V_i$ and $V_j$ form an $(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular pair in $G$ in the sense of \noderef{SuperRegularPair}.
\end{definition}
