\begin{lemma}[Super-regularity under a part embedding]
Let $e:V\to W$ be injective, $G,H$ graphs on $V,W$, and $L,R$ finite
parts of $V$. Suppose $H(e(x),e(y))$ holds if and only if $G(x,y)$
for every $x\in L$, $y\in R$. If $(G;L,R)$ is
$(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular, then $(H;e(L),e(R))$ is
$(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular. No surjectivity on the ambient type
is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By injectivity, $|e(S)|=|S|$ for every finite $S\subseteq V$.
For $P\subseteq L$, $Q\subseteq R$, the map $(x,y)\mapsto(e(x),e(y))$
is a bijection between their ordered edge sets: injectivity gives
uniqueness of preimages, and the assumed adjacency equivalence gives
both edge preservation and edge reflection. Hence the edge counts
and, by \noderef{PairDensity}, the densities of these rectangles agree.
For arbitrary $P'\subseteq e(L)$, $Q'\subseteq e(R)$, take
$P=\{x\in L:e(x)\in P'\}$ and $Q=\{y\in R:e(y)\in Q'\}$.
Their images are exactly $P',Q'$. Their sizes equal the sizes of those
images, so the strict rectangle-size hypotheses in
\noderef{RegularPair} pull back exactly. Apply the regularity clause
of \noderef{SuperRegularPair} and the density identity to obtain both
density bounds on $P',Q'$.
For a left vertex $e(x)$ with $x\in L$, the map $y\mapsto e(y)$ is
a bijection from its neighbours in $R$ to the neighbours of $e(x)$
in $e(R)$. The right-neighbour count is identical. Likewise, for
$e(y)$ with $y\in R$, its neighbours in $e(L)$ correspond exactly
to the neighbours of $y$ in $L$. Applying the two degree clauses of
\noderef{SuperRegularPair}, and replacing the part sizes by their equal
image sizes, proves both degree windows and completes the proof.
\end{proof}
