\begin{lemma}[Near-equal varying-error simultaneous good-edge retention]
Let $W_1,W_2$ be disjoint sets of size $m$, and let a finite set $K$ index
sets $W_3^k$ with $|W_3^k|\in\{m-1,m,m+1\}$, each disjoint from $W_1,W_2$.
Suppose $F'_{12}[W_1,W_2]$ is $(\varepsilon_0,\delta)$-super-regular and,
for every $k$, the pairs $F_{13}^k[W_1,W_3^k]$ and
$F_{23}^k[W_2,W_3^k]$ are $(\varepsilon_k,d_{13}^k)$- and
$(\varepsilon_k,d_{23}^k)$-super-regular. Assume
$\varepsilon_k\leq1$, $2\varepsilon_k<d_{13}^k,d_{23}^k\leq1$, and
\[
 \varepsilon_0\leq\eta,\qquad2\sqrt{\varepsilon_k}\leq\eta,
 \qquad \varepsilon_0+\sum_k\sqrt{\varepsilon_k}\leq\eta,
 \qquad \varepsilon_0+2\sum_k\varepsilon_k\leq\eta.
\]
Retain an edge $xv$ of $F'_{12}$ precisely when, for every $k$,
\[
 |N_{F_{13}^k}(x)\cap N_{F_{23}^k}(v)\cap W_3^k|
 =(d_{13}^kd_{23}^k\pm2\varepsilon_k)|W_3^k|.
\]
Then the retained graph on $(W_1,W_2)$ is $(\eta,\delta)$-super-regular.
Every displayed window is normalized by the actual part $W_3^k$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Call the retained graph $F$.  Its defining relation makes it a subgraph of
$F'_{12}$ supported on $W_1\times W_2$.

Fix $x\in W_1$ and $k\in K$, and put
$S=N_{F_{13}^k}(x)\cap W_3^k$.  Super-regularity gives
$|S|=(d_{13}^k\pm\varepsilon_k)|W_3^k|$ and
$|S|>\varepsilon_k|W_3^k|$, since $2\varepsilon_k<d_{13}^k$.
Regularity of $F_{23}^k[W_2,W_3^k]$ implies that fewer than
$\varepsilon_k|W_2|$ vertices of $W_2$ have degree into $S$ below
$(d_{23}^k-\varepsilon_k)|S|$, and fewer than that many have degree above
$(d_{23}^k+\varepsilon_k)|S|$: either contrary exceptional set, together
with $S$, would violate regularity.  Every remaining $v$ therefore has
\[
 |N_{F_{13}^k}(x)\cap N_{F_{23}^k}(v)\cap W_3^k|
 =(d_{13}^kd_{23}^k\pm2\varepsilon_k)|W_3^k|,
\]
using only that both densities are at most one.  Thus the $k$th test deletes
at most $2\varepsilon_k|W_2|$ partners of $x$.  The symmetric argument for
fixed $v\in W_2$ deletes at most $2\varepsilon_k|W_1|$ partners.  Summing
over $k$, and then using
$\varepsilon_0+2\sum_k\varepsilon_k\leq\eta$, converts the original
$(\delta\pm\varepsilon_0)$ degree windows into
$(\delta\pm\eta)$ windows on both sides.  Notice that no conversion between
$m$ and $|W_3^k|$ occurred.

For regularity, take $U\subseteq W_1$ and $Z\subseteq W_2$ with
$|U|>\eta|W_1|$ and $|Z|>\eta|W_2|$.  For fixed $k$, apply
\noderef{OneIndexBadPairCounting} to the two super-regular pairs with third
part $W_3^k$.  Its proof and statement normalize the two endpoint sets by
$|W_1|=|W_2|=m$ and all third-part degrees by the actual $|W_3^k|$; hence
the hypothesis $2\sqrt{\varepsilon_k}\leq\eta$ shows that at most a
$\sqrt{\varepsilon_k}$ proportion of $U\times Z$ fails the $k$th displayed
test.  A union bound gives
\[
 0\leq d_{F'_{12}}(U,Z)-d_F(U,Z)
 \leq\sum_k\sqrt{\varepsilon_k}.
\]
Apply \noderef{RegularityAfterDensityLoss} with this last sum as the density
loss.  Its hypotheses are exactly $\varepsilon_0\leq\eta$ and
$\varepsilon_0+\sum_k\sqrt{\varepsilon_k}\leq\eta$, so $F$ is
$(\eta,\delta)$-regular.  Together with the two degree calculations this is
precisely $(\eta,\delta)$-super-regularity.
\end{proof}
