\begin{lemma}[Threshold transfer to restricted parts]
Let $(U,W)$ in $G$ be lower $(\varepsilon,d)$-regular,
$U'\subseteq U$, $W'\subseteq W$, and $\varepsilon\leq\theta$.
Assume $\varepsilon|U|\leq\theta|U'|$ and
$\varepsilon|W|\leq\theta|W'|$. Then $(U',W')$ is lower
$(\theta,d)$-regular. The parameters may be extended nonnegative
reals and all subtraction is truncated.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For any $S\subseteq U'$, $T\subseteq W'$ above the strict
$\theta$ thresholds, the assumptions imply
$|S|>\varepsilon|U|$ and $|T|>\varepsilon|W|$.
Apply the definition in \noderef{LowerRegularPair} to these sets
inside $U,W$. It gives density at least $d-\varepsilon$.
Monotonicity of truncated subtraction and $\varepsilon\leq\theta$
give $d-\theta\leq d-\varepsilon$, proving the required lower bound.
This proof makes no positivity, finiteness, or nonempty-set assumption
beyond the displayed strict tests.
\end{proof}
