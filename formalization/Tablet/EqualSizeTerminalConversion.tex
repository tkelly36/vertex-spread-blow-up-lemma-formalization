\begin{lemma}[Full-domain terminal conversion]
Let $B$ be a finite blow-up instance with candidacy system $A$, let
$X\subseteq V_H$ be a finite set with $X=V_H$, and let $\mu$ be any law on
\noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding} with domain $X$. Suppose the law of
the restricted maps $x\in X\mapsto f(x)$ is $b$-vertex-spread on the whole
host vertex set in the sense of \noderef{VertexSpreadOn}.
There is a map $F$ from these partial embeddings to full injective graph
homomorphisms $H\hookrightarrow G$ satisfying \noderef{CompatibleEmbedding},
such that $F(f)(x)=f(x)$ for every $f$ and every $x\in V_H$.
The pushforward $\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{map}}F$ has $b$-vertex-spread
underlying maps on the whole host vertex set.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every source vertex belongs to $X$. For any partial embedding $f$,
injectivity on $X$ from \noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding} is therefore
global injectivity. Its edge-preservation field applies to every source
edge and supplies a graph homomorphism into $G$. Together these form a
\texttt{SimpleGraph.Copy}; no reflection of host edges is asserted or
needed. The compatibility field applies to every source vertex and is
exactly \noderef{CompatibleEmbedding}. Define $F(f)$ by these fields
with underlying function $f.toFun$. This constructs $F$ on every input,
not only on the support of $\mu$, and proves the displayed pointwise equality.

Let $e:X\simeq V_H$ be the bijection given by inclusion; its inverse
sends $x$ to $\langle x,x\in X\rangle$, using $X=V_H$.
The underlying full map of $F(f)$ is the restricted map of $f$ composed
with $e^{-1}$. Fix $s\in\mathbb N$, an injective map
$x:\operatorname{Fin}(s)\to V_H$, and any prescribed host map
$v:\operatorname{Fin}(s)\to V_G$. The map $e^{-1}\circ x$ is injective.
For each input $f$, the conditions
\[
 F(f)(x(k))=v(k)\quad\text{for every }k
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 f(e^{-1}(x(k)))=v(k)\quad\text{for every }k
\]
are identical. By \noderef{CylinderEvent}, these cylinders have the
same inverse image in the law on partial embeddings. The pushforward
mass formula, summing the original masses over each fibre, therefore
gives equal cylinder probabilities, including when $s=0$.
The assumed restriction-law bound gives probability at most $b^s$.
Also $b\leq1$ is part of the same \noderef{VertexSpreadOn} assumption.
These are precisely the two required clauses for the full underlying-map
law of $\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{map}}F$. Thus both maps and spread are
preserved, independently of how the full-domain law was obtained.
\end{proof}
