\begin{lemma}[Simultaneous choices for one colour round]
Fix $0<d\leq1$, $\Delta\in\mathbb N$, total exposure sequences $p,\rho$,
and $0<e\leq1$. Assume $0<p_x\leq1$, $0<\rho_x<p_x$ for
$x\leq\Delta$, and $p_y-p_{y+1}>0$ for $y<\Delta$.
There are scalars $\omega,\theta,\zeta,\widehat\zeta,b$ and a
certificate $K$ in the sense of \noderef{InductionApplicationCertificate},
with degree bound $\Delta$, certified round bound $1$, host density $d$,
and parameter sequences
\[
 t_s=\theta,\quad D_x=p_x,\quad \beta_s=b,\quad
 \rho_x=\rho_x,\quad \eta_s=\zeta,\quad
 \omega_s=\omega,\quad \xi_s=\widehat\zeta.
\]
They satisfy
\[
 0<b<\widehat\zeta<\zeta,\quad 0<\theta,\quad
 0<\omega<e,\quad b\leq e,
\]
\[
 2\sqrt\zeta\leq\theta,\quad
 \zeta+\Delta\sqrt\zeta\leq\theta,\quad
 \zeta+2\Delta\zeta\leq\theta,
\]
and, for every $x\leq\Delta$,
\[
 4\theta<\rho_x,\quad 2\zeta<\rho_x,\quad
 2\zeta<1-\rho_x.
\]
Also $2\zeta<d/2$, $2\zeta<1-d/2$, and
$2b<p_y-p_{y+1}$ for every $y<\Delta$.
Every $K$-matching constant at round zero and exposure $x\leq\Delta$
is finite. Thus $K$ contains actual witness and host thinnings at input
error $b$, and Four Graphs data for every ordered pair $x,y\leq\Delta$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All quantities used below are finite real numbers: $d\leq1$,
$0<\rho_x<p_x\leq1$, and $0<e\leq1$.
In particular $0<d/2<1$ and $0<\rho_x<1$.
For each ordered pair $x,y\leq\Delta$, apply
\noderef{NearEqualFourGraphs} to $(\rho_x,\rho_y,d/2)$.
Its six density hypotheses hold by these inequalities. Write $u_{xy}>0$
for its output-error cap. Take $\omega$ to be one half of the minimum
of $e$, $1$, and all $u_{xy}$ (allowing an infinite cap does not affect
this minimum). The index set is finite and nonempty, including when
$\Delta=0$. Hence $0<\omega<e$ and $\omega\leq u_{xy}$.
Only now use the output-error universal quantifier in Four Graphs:
for each pair it supplies a positive input-error cap $v_{xy}$ at
this fixed $\omega$.

Let $\theta$ be one half of the minimum of $1$, all $v_{xy}$,
and all $\rho_x/8$. Then $\theta>0$, $\theta\leq v_{xy}$, and
$4\theta\leq\rho_x/4<\rho_x$.
At this fixed input error, Four Graphs supplies an exponential-constant
cap $c_{xy}>0$. Set $a_{xy}=\min(1,c_{xy})/2$.
Thus $0<a_{xy}\leq1$ and $a_{xy}\leq c_{xy}$; apply its last
universal quantifier at $a_{xy}$ and record the resulting order threshold
and probability guarantee. This follows the output-error, input-error,
exponential-constant quantifier order exactly. Both exposure coordinates
range independently over the full set $\{0,\ldots,\Delta\}$.

For each $x\leq\Delta$, apply \noderef{UniformPerfectMatchingSpread}
at $(\theta,\rho_x)$: $\theta>0$, $4\theta<\rho_x$, and
$\rho_x\leq1$ verify its hypotheses. Record its finite constant
$M_x\geq1$, threshold, and spread guarantee. Its map to matching
functions is exactly the map to ambient-vertex-valued functions in the
certificate, after composing with the right-subtype inclusion.
Explicitly, unfold \noderef{VertexSpreadOn} and \noderef{CylinderEvent}.
For each cylinder of ambient prescriptions $y_i$, if any
$y_i$ is outside the right part its mass is zero; otherwise form the
right-subtype prescriptions with these values. Their cylinder is the
preimage of the ambient cylinder under the inclusion, so the same
$(M_x/m)^k$ bound holds. The constraint $M_x/m\leq1$ is unchanged.
Apply \noderef{MatchingEnumeration} at $(\theta,\rho_x)$, since
$2\theta\leq4\theta<\rho_x$. Its lower bound
$(\rho_x-2\theta)^m m!$ is positive: the base is positive and finite,
its natural power is positive also for $m=0$, and $m!>0$.
Consequently the finite set of perfect matchings has positive cardinality,
so a matching exists. Record this threshold and existence guarantee.

Choose a positive real $h$ strictly below each of
\[
 1,\quad\frac{\theta}{2(\Delta+1)},\quad
 \frac{\theta}{1+2\Delta},\quad
 \min_{x\leq\Delta}\frac{\rho_x}{20},\quad
 \min_{x\leq\Delta}\frac{1-\rho_x}{4},\quad
 \frac{d/2}{20},\quad\frac{1-d/2}{4}.
\]
Every entry is positive, so half their minimum is a valid explicit
choice. Set $\zeta=h^2$ and $\widehat\zeta=\zeta/2$.
Since $0<h<1$, $\zeta\leq h$ and $\sqrt\zeta=h$.
It follows that
\[
 2\sqrt\zeta=2h<\theta,\qquad
 \zeta+\Delta\sqrt\zeta\leq(\Delta+1)h<\theta,\qquad
 \zeta+2\Delta\zeta\leq(1+2\Delta)h<\theta.
\]
Also $2\zeta\leq2h<\rho_x,1-\rho_x,d/2,1-d/2$, and
$10\widehat\zeta=5h^2\leq5h<\rho_x,d/2$.
These verify all the claimed padding reserves, including the strict
inner gap $\zeta-\widehat\zeta>0$.

Let $b$ be half the minimum of $e$, $\widehat\zeta/16$, and the
finitely many $(p_y-p_{y+1})/4$ for $y<\Delta$.
When $\Delta=0$ omit the last family. Thus
$0<b<\widehat\zeta$, $b\leq e$, and
$2b\leq(p_y-p_{y+1})/4<p_y-p_{y+1}$.
Put $\xi=\widehat\zeta/16>0$.
For each witness take $(D,\rho)=(p_x,\rho_x)$; for each host take
$(D,\rho)=(d,d/2)$. In each case $0<\rho<D\leq1$ and
$\rho/D\leq1$; division is ordinary division because $D$ is finite
and positive. Hence
\[
 4\bigl((\rho/D)b+\xi\bigr)
 \leq4\bigl(\widehat\zeta/16+\widehat\zeta/16\bigr)
 =\widehat\zeta/2\leq\widehat\zeta.
\]
Together with $0<b<\widehat\zeta$, $\xi>0$, and
$10\widehat\zeta<\rho$, this checks every scalar hypothesis of
\noderef{QuantitativeSuperRegularThinning}. Apply it separately to
each witness density and to the host density, and record the resulting
thresholds and actual spanning-subgraph conclusions, including their
disjointness, two size cutoffs, and both near-equality assumptions.

Use the recorded data at round zero to fill the fields of $K$:
the thinning thresholds and conclusions just obtained, the constants
$a_{xy}$ and their Four Graphs thresholds and conclusions, and the
constants $M_x$, matching thresholds, existence thresholds, and their
guarantees. All fields are required only at round zero and exposures
at most $\Delta$. Extend the data functions elsewhere by zero thresholds,
Four Graphs constant $1/2$, and matching constant $1$; no guarantee is
required there. The two constant comparison fields hold because
$0<a_{xy}\leq1$ and $M_x\geq1$. This constructs exactly
\noderef{InductionApplicationCertificate} with the displayed parameters.
All graph guarantees are uniform in finite vertex types. One may first
choose their scalar data at the universe of the intended certificate
(including finite sums and subtype copies); the statements impose no
vertex-count dependence on these choices. These are applications of the
displayed universal graph guarantees, not transport of an auxiliary
proof-local result. None of the choices uses $r$.
\end{proof}
