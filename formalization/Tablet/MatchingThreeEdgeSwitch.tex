\begin{definition}[Three-edge switching map]
Let $A,B$ be sets, $f:A\to B$, and $x,a,b\in A$. Define
$S(f;x,a,b)=f\circ\tau_{x,a}\circ\tau_{a,b}$, where $\tau_{u,v}$
interchanges $u,v$ and fixes all other elements (and is the identity if
$u=v$). For a matching represented by $f$ and distinct $x,a,b$, this is
the candidate operation replacing the three matching edges by the other
three edges of an alternating six-cycle, when those replacement edges
are present in the graph.
\end{definition}
