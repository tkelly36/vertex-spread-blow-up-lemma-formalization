\begin{definition}
For a simple graph $G$ on an arbitrary vertex type and arbitrary finite vertex subsets $U,W$, let $e_G(U,W)$ be the number of ordered pairs $(u,w)\in U\times W$ with $uw\in E(G)$.  The sets may overlap.  The density of the ordered pair is
\[
d_G(U,W)=\frac{e_G(U,W)}{|U||W|},
\]
with value $0$ when either part is empty.  The regularity applications in the paper specialize this definition to disjoint parts.
\end{definition}
