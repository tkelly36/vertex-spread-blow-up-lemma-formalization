\begin{definition}
For a blow-up instance with candidacy system \noderef{CandidacySystem}, the candidacy graphs are lower-$(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular if, for every cluster index $j$, the bipartite graph $A^j$ of allowed pairs between $X_j$ and $V_j$ is lower-$(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular in the sense of \noderef{LowerSuperRegularPair}.
\end{definition}
