\begin{definition}
For a blow-up instance, a partial compatible embedding $f$ on $X$, and $i\in[r]$, the restricted updated candidacy graph $A_f^i$ is the relation consisting of the edges of \noderef{UpdatedCandidacy} whose source endpoint lies in $X_i$ and whose host endpoint lies in $V_i$.  Equivalently, it is the subgraph of $A_f$ induced between $X_i\setminus X$ and $V_i\setminus f(X)$.
\end{definition}
