\begin{definition}
Let $G$ be a simple graph on an arbitrary vertex type, let $V_1,V_2$ be arbitrary finite vertex subsets, and let $\varepsilon,d$ be arbitrary extended nonnegative reals.  The ordered pair $(V_1,V_2)$ is lower $(\varepsilon,d)$-regular if every finite $U\subseteq V_1$ and $W\subseteq V_2$ satisfying $|U|>\varepsilon|V_1|$ and $|W|>\varepsilon|V_2|$ has $d_G(U,W)\geq d-\varepsilon$, where density is as in \noderef{PairDensity}.  The paper uses this predicate for finite ambient graphs, disjoint cluster fibers, $\varepsilon>0$, and $0<d<1$.
\end{definition}
