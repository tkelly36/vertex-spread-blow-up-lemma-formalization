\begin{lemma}[Lower super-regularity under complete-row padding]
Let $L,Q$ be finite vertex sets of a simple graph $F$, let $e$ inject its vertex set into that of a simple graph $F^+$, and let $D$ be a finite vertex set disjoint from $e(L)$. Let $\varepsilon,\eta,d$ be extended nonnegative reals. Suppose $|e(L)\cup D|=|Q|$, adjacency between $e(L)$ and $e(Q)$ agrees exactly with adjacency between $L$ and $Q$, and every vertex of $D$ is adjacent to every vertex of $e(Q)$. If $(L,Q)$ is lower-$(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular, $0<\varepsilon\leq\eta^2$, $\eta\leq1$, and $d\leq1$, then $(e(L)\cup D,e(Q))$ is lower-$(\eta,d)$-super-regular.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $M=|L|$, $K=|D|$, and $N=|Q|=M+K$. The budgets give $0<\eta$ and $\varepsilon\leq\eta$. If $d\leq\eta$, all required lower bounds are zero, so assume $\eta<d$. All parameters are then finite.

For tested subsets $S\subseteq e(L)\cup D$ and $T\subseteq e(Q)$, write $S=e(S_0)\cup S_D$ disjointly and $T=e(T_0)$, with $S_0\subseteq L$, $S_D\subseteq D$, and $T_0\subseteq Q$. Their thresholds are $|S|>\eta N$ and $|T_0|>\eta N$, so both cardinalities are positive. Exact adjacency and complete dummy rows give the edge count
$E_{F^+}(S,T)=E_F(S_0,T_0)+|S_D||T_0|$.
If $|S_0|>\varepsilon M$, the right threshold also exceeds $\varepsilon N$. The lower-regularity clause of \noderef{LowerSuperRegularPair}, expressed by \noderef{LowerRegularPair} and \noderef{PairDensity}, gives $E_F(S_0,T_0)\geq(d-\varepsilon)|S_0||T_0|$. As $d-\eta\leq d-\varepsilon$ and $d-\eta\leq1$, the displayed edge count is at least $(d-\eta)|S||T_0|$.
Otherwise $|S_0|\leq\varepsilon M\leq\varepsilon N\leq\eta^2N<\eta|S|$. Consequently $|S_D|\geq(1-\eta)|S|\geq(d-\eta)|S|$, and the complete rows alone give the same bound. Divide by the positive finite rectangle cardinality to obtain lower regularity. Empty parts admit no tested rectangle and require no division.

For an old left vertex its degree is unchanged and at least $(d-\varepsilon)N\geq(d-\eta)N$; a dummy has degree $N$. A right vertex has its old degree plus $K$, at least $(d-\varepsilon)M+K\geq(d-\eta)(M+K)$, because the difference is $(\eta-\varepsilon)M+(1-d+\eta)K\geq0$. These are exactly the two degree clauses of \noderef{LowerSuperRegularPair}.
\end{proof}
