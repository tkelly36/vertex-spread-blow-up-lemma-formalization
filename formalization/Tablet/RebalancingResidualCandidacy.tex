\begin{lemma}[Residual candidacy with exceptional rows]
Let $B,A$ be a finite blow-up instance with equal source and host fiber
sizes and lower-$(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regular candidacy. Let $f$ be a
partial compatible embedding on $X$, and let $B_r,A_r$ live on the
literal complement subtypes with induced graphs, unchanged reduced
graph, restricted cluster maps, and allowed relation exactly $A_f$.
Suppose both residual fibers have size $m$. Put
$E=\{x\notin X:\exists y\in X,\ xy\in E(H)\}$.
Suppose the deleted source and host counts in every fiber are at most
$L$, the count $|E\cap X_i|$ is at most $J$, and $J\leq\eta m$.
Assume $0<\varepsilon'<\infty$ and, for every $i$,
\[
 \varepsilon|V_i|\leq\varepsilon'm,\qquad
 (d'-\varepsilon')+\eta/\varepsilon'\leq d-\varepsilon,\qquad
 (d'-\varepsilon')m\leq(d-\varepsilon)|V_i|-L-J.
\]
Finally suppose $(d'-\varepsilon')m\leq\rho$ and every $x\in E$ has
at least $\rho$ updated candidates in the original host universe.
Then $A_r$ is lower-$(\varepsilon',d')$-super-regular candidacy for $B_r$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix $i$, and use the sum-vertex representation in
\noderef{CandidacyLowerSuperRegular}. The original left and right parts
are the copies of $X_i$ and $V_i$ in $V(H)\oplus V(G)$. They form a
lower-super-regular pair by assumption. Let $D_L$ be the left copy of
$X\cap X_i$, $D_R$ the right copy of $f(X)\cap V_i$, and let $E_i$ be the
left copy of $E\cap X_i$. These sets lie in the asserted original parts;
$E_i$ is disjoint from $D_L$ because the definition of $E$ excludes $X$.
Define the updated sum-vertex graph by the relation $A_f$ in
\noderef{UpdatedCandidacy}. For opposite tags its adjacency is exactly
that allowed relation: the forward relation supplies the edge, the
reverse relation has impossible tags, and unequal tags exclude loops.
This observation applies also to the original candidacy graph.

For $x\in X_i\setminus(X\cup E)$, no vertex of $X$ is a neighbor of $x$.
For unused $v\in V_i\setminus f(X)$, expanding the updated relation
therefore gives $A_f(x,v)\iff A(x,v)$: both exclusions hold and every
extra neighbor condition is vacuous. This verifies both directions of
the ordinary-row equality required by
\noderef{ExceptionalLeftLowerSuperRegular}.
For $x\in E\cap X_i$, every updated candidate lies in
$V_i\setminus f(X)$, by the original-candidacy and unused-host clauses
of the updated relation and cluster respect in \noderef{CandidacySystem}.
Hence the entire candidate set is bijective with its updated neighbors
in the residual right part. The reserve hypothesis and
$(d'-\varepsilon')m\leq\rho$ give the required exceptional-row degree.

The two ambient residual parts have size $m$: the literal subtype
projections and the restricted-map identities identify them with the
residual fibers. Since $|X_i|=|V_i|$, the displayed threshold assumption
transfers both thresholds. The exceptional fraction follows from
$|E_i|\leq J\leq\eta m$. The density margin is an assumption.
For the left-degree margin, the stronger assumed degree inequality
implies $(d'-\varepsilon')m\leq(d-\varepsilon)|V_i|-L$ by deleting
the nonnegative $J$ loss, and replacing $L$ by $|D_R|\leq L$ weakens
the required bound. For the right-degree margin use $|X_i|=|V_i|$ and
$|D_L|\leq L$, $|E_i|\leq J$ to get
\[
 (d'-\varepsilon')m\leq(d-\varepsilon)|X_i|-|D_L|-|E_i|.
\]
Antitonicity of truncated subtraction justifies both replacements.
All hypotheses of \noderef{ExceptionalLeftLowerSuperRegular} have thus
been verified, giving lower-super-regularity of this ambient updated pair.

Finally project the sum of the two complement subtypes into the original
sum universe, preserving tags. This is injective and sends each residual
fiber exactly onto its ambient residual part. On opposite tags the
assumed exact relation identifies adjacency in both directions. On equal
tags both graphs have no edges. Thus the product projection bijects
filtered ordered edge sets, and projections biject filtered neighborhoods.
Their cardinalities, and the denominators in \noderef{PairDensity}, agree.
Both strict regularity thresholds and both degree clauses in
\noderef{LowerSuperRegularPair} consequently transfer without change.
This proves \noderef{CandidacyLowerSuperRegular} for $A_r$ in every fiber.
\end{proof}
