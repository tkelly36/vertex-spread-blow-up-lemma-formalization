\begin{helper}[Quadratic bound for a negative exponential parameter]
For every real $\lambda\le0$,
\[
 e^\lambda-1-\lambda\le\lambda^2/2.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Put $q(x)=x^2/2-e^x+1+x$. This function is differentiable on the
real line and $q'(x)=x+1-e^x$. The elementary exponential tangent
inequality $1+x\le e^x$ gives $q'(x)\le0$ for every real $x$.
The mean value theorem therefore makes $q$ antitone on the real line.
Since $\lambda\le0$, $q(\lambda)\ge q(0)=0$. Expanding this last
inequality gives the asserted bound.
\end{proof}
