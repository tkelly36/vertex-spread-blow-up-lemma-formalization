\begin{definition}
For arbitrary types $X,Y$, a natural number $s$, and maps $x:\operatorname{Fin}(s)\to X$ and $y:\operatorname{Fin}(s)\to Y$, the cylinder event $\operatorname{Cyl}(x,y)$ is the set of all functions $f:X\to Y$ such that $f(x(i))=y(i)$ for every $i\in\operatorname{Fin}(s)$.  No finiteness or distinctness assumption is part of this definition.  The vertex-spread application in the paper specializes it to finite $X,Y$ and distinct entries of $x$.
\end{definition}
