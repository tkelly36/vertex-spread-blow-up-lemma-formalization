\begin{lemma}[Net flow across a cut]
Let $f$ be a feasible integral flow on a finite network with distinct
terminals, as in \noderef{FiniteIntegralFlow}. For every subset $S$ with
$s\in S$ and $t\notin S$,
\[
 |f|=\sum_{v\in S}\sum_{w\notin S} f(v,w).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Sum the divergence $\sum_w f(v,w)$ over $v\in S$. By conservation in
\noderef{FiniteIntegralFlow}, each term except the source term is zero:
the sink is outside $S$, and any other vertex in $S$ is neither terminal.
The sum is therefore $|f|$. Split each inner sum into $w\in S$ and
$w\notin S$. The sum over $S\times S$ is zero: the bijection
$(v,w)\mapsto(w,v)$ negates every summand by skew symmetry, so this
integer sum is its own negative and hence zero. Equivalently diagonal
terms are zero and off-diagonal terms cancel in pairs. Removing that
sum gives the asserted identity.
\end{proof}
