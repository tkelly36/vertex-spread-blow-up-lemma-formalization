\begin{definition}
Let $f$ be a partial compatible embedding on $X$ in the sense of \noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding}.  The updated candidacy system $A_f$ has an allowed pair $(x,v)$ exactly when $x\notin X$, $v\notin f(X)$, the original system allows $(x,v)$, and $v$ is adjacent in $G$ to $f(x')$ for every already embedded neighbor $x'\in X\cap N_H(x)$.  Thus it is the paper's updated candidacy graph on the unused source and host vertices.
\end{definition}
