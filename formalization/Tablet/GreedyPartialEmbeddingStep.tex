\begin{lemma}[One-vertex greedy extension]
Let $\Delta,n$ be natural numbers with $n>0$, and let $d,\varepsilon,a$
be extended nonnegative reals satisfying
\[
 0<a,\quad a\leq d(d-\varepsilon)_+^k\ (k\leq\Delta),\quad
 \varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)<a,\quad
 (\Delta+1)\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)\leq a/2.
\]
Let $B,A$ be a finite blow-up instance with candidacy system, whose
host is lower-$(\varepsilon,d)$-regular, with
$\Delta(H)\leq\Delta$, $|X_i|\leq\varepsilon|V_i|$, and
$|V_i|\leq(1+\varepsilon)n$ for every $i$. For every greedy state
$f$ on $S$ and every $z\notin S$, there is a nonempty finite set $L$
of at least $(a/2)n$ possible images such that each $v\in L$ gives a
greedy state on $S\cup\{z\}$ whose total map is exactly $f$ with its
value at $z$ replaced by $v$. Greedy states are those of
\noderef{GreedyPartialEmbedding}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $b=(d-\varepsilon)_+$ and $i=c(z)$. The exposure count
$k_S(x)=|S\cap N_H(x)|$ is at most $\Delta$. Hence
\noderef{GreedyPartialEmbedding} and the power hypothesis give
$|D_S^f(x)|\geq an$ for every $x\notin S$. By
\noderef{RawCandidates} and \noderef{CandidacySystem}, this set lies
in $V_{c(x)}$.
For each unembedded neighbor $w$ of $z$, put $j=c(w)$ and
\[
 E_w=\{v\in V_i:|N_G(v)\cap D_S^f(w)|<b|D_S^f(w)|\}.
\]
We have $|D_S^f(w)|\geq an>\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)n
\geq\varepsilon|V_j|$. The strict inequality follows by multiplying
the strict threshold hypothesis by the positive finite integer $n$.
The edge $zw$ gives $R(i,j)$ by \noderef{BlowUpInstance}.
Thus \noderef{InstanceLowerRegular} supplies the lower-regular pair
$(V_i,V_j)$, and \noderef{LowerRegularBadRows} gives
$|E_w|\leq\varepsilon|V_i|$. This uses only lower regularity.

Let $O_i=f(S)\cap V_i$. On $S$ the map is injective and compatible
by \noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding}. Cluster respect implies
that an image lies in $V_i$ exactly when its source lies in $X_i$.
Thus $|O_i|=|S\cap X_i|\leq|X_i|\leq\varepsilon|V_i|$.
Define
\[
 L=D_S^f(z)\setminus\left(O_i\cup
                      \bigcup_{w\in N_H(z)\setminus S}E_w\right).
\]
There are at most $\Delta$ bad sets. Subtracting the size of a union
of forbidden sets from the size of $D_S^f(z)$ gives
\[
 |L|\geq an-(\Delta+1)\varepsilon|V_i|
 \geq an-(\Delta+1)\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)n\geq(a/2)n.
\]
All quantities subtracted here are finite. Indeed, if $a$ were infinite,
$an\leq|D_S^f(z)|$ would contradict finiteness of this raw set.
If $\varepsilon$ were infinite, the strict threshold hypothesis would
fail. The loss inequality is therefore ordinary subtraction of finite
nonnegative real quantities. Since $a>0$ and $n>0$, the last lower
bound is positive and $L$ is nonempty.

Fix $v\in L$ and replace the value at $z$ by $v$. Raw membership
gives $A(z,v)$ and every required edge to an embedded neighbor of $z$.
It also places $v$ in $V_i$. Because $v\notin O_i$, it is outside
$f(S)$: any image equal to $v$ necessarily lies in $V_i$. Thus the
updated map is injective on $S\cup\{z\}$, remains compatible, and
preserves all edges of the induced source graph. Values on $S$ did
not change. For each remaining vertex $w$ adjacent to $z$, its new
raw set is exactly $D_S^f(w)\cap N_G(v)$ and its new exposure count
is $k_S(w)+1$. Because $v\notin E_w$, its size is at least
\[
 b|D_S^f(w)|\geq d b^{k_S(w)+1}n.
\]
Multiplication is monotone in the extended nonnegative reals, including
zero endpoints. A remaining vertex not adjacent to $z$ has exactly
the same raw set and exposure count as before. Hence the invariant
holds for every remaining vertex, not just vertices of a prescribed
future embedded set. Together with partial compatibility this is
precisely the required greedy state.
\end{proof}
