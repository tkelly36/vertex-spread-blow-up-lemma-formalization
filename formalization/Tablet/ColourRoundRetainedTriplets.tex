\begin{lemma}[Deterministic retained triplet family]
Let $B,A,c,\theta,p,\varepsilon,\rho,\zeta,\omega,\xi,D,d,C,a,n,t$
be a blow-up instance, candidacy system, colouring, parameter sequences and
scalars, with a \noderef{ColourRoundStepParameters} record at these parameters
and $n\ge1$. Assume each source cluster has size $n$ or $n+1$ and the same
size as its host cluster; the original host pairs are
$(\varepsilon_0,d)$-super-regular; every reduced edge is a
\noderef{SaturatingSourcePair}; and
\noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} holds for $(t,S,X,\mu)$.
Fix $i$ with $c(i)=t$, $i\notin S$, and $f\in\operatorname{supp}\mu$.
Write $Y=X_i$, $K_0=V_i$, $U=\{\mathrm{inl}(y):y\in Y\}$,
$K=\{\mathrm{inr}(v):v\in K_0\}$, and
\[
 \mathcal J=\{j:ij\in E(R),\ j\notin S,\ c(j)\ge t\},\qquad
 Z_j=\{\mathrm{inr}(v):v\in V_j\},\quad
 V'_j=\{\mathrm{inl}(y):y\in X_j\}.
\]
Let $e_j=|N_R(j)\cap(\{k:c(k)<t\}\cup S)|$, and let $A_f^j$ be
the graph on the ambient source/host sum whose edges are precisely the
symmetrized pairs $\mathrm{inl}(y)\mathrm{inr}(v)$ with
$(y,v)\in\noderef{UpdatedCandidacyCluster}$ at $(B,A,X,f,j)$.
There are graphs $F,F_{13}^j,F_{23}^j$ on this ambient sum such that
$F\subseteq A_f^i$, the pair $(F;U,K)$ is
$(\theta_t,\rho_{e_i})$-super-regular, and for every $j\in\mathcal J$
the three graphs form a \noderef{NearEqualRegularTriplet} on
$(U,K,Z_j)$ at size $|Y|$ with parameters
$(\theta_t,\rho_{e_i},\rho_{e_j},d/2)$.
Moreover, for any bijection $\sigma:U\to K$, if its matching-induced
intersection of $F_{13}^j$ and $F_{23}^j$ on the canonical subtype copies
of $U,Z_j$ is $(\omega_{t+1},\rho_{e_j}d/2)$-super-regular, there is a graph
$W_j$ on the ambient sum with
\[
 W_j\subseteq T^j_\sigma,\qquad
 (W_j;V'_j,Z_j)\text{ is }(\varepsilon_{t+1},\rho_{e_j}d/2)
 \text{-super-regular}.
\]
Here $T^j_\sigma$ is the symmetrization of the relation
\[
 \mathrm{inl}(y),\mathrm{inr}(v):\quad
 \mathrm{inl}(y)\mathrm{inr}(v)\in E(A_f^j),\quad
 \forall u\in U,\quad
 \bigl[u=\mathrm{inl}(x),\ xy\in E(H)\bigr]
 \Longrightarrow
 \bigl[\sigma(u)=\mathrm{inr}(w),\ wv\in E(G)\bigr].
\]
This conclusion is deterministic; it asserts no matching probability law.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{ColourRoundWitnessThinnings} with the given instance,
parameters, invariant and supported partial embedding. Write $x=e_i$.
It supplies $x\le D$, independently $e_j<D$ for every $j\in\mathcal J$,
a contained current pair $W_{12}$ of parameters $(\xi_t,\rho_x)$,
and future families $O_j,W_j,J_j$. On the full future parts,
$O_j\subseteq A_f^j$ has parameters $(\varepsilon_t,p_{e_j})$,
$W_j\subseteq O_j$ has parameters $(\xi_t,\rho_{e_j})$, and
$J_j\subseteq H_0$ on the full host parts has parameters $(\xi_t,d/2)$.
This helper proves the exact witness-part identities, both threshold
bounds at every application, and the universe and host-copy adapters.
It uses the supplied guarantees, not an assertion of certificate existence.

For each $j\in\mathcal J$, apply
\noderef{ColourRoundSourceWitnessAssembly}, with $y=e_j<D$,
the given saturating source pair, the supplied cluster sizes, and
$O_j,W_j,J_j$. It gives a graph $F_{13}^j$ on $U,Z_j$ with parameters
$(\zeta_t,\rho_{e_j})$, and the precise deterministic transport into
$T^j_\sigma$ in the conclusion, for every successful bijection $\sigma$.
The source adapter treats both injection orientations, the two
one-point complements, artificial-row deletion, and genuine-row
restoration using the stored old witness. Choose these finitely many
test graphs together, and set $F_{23}^j=J_j$.

We now prove simultaneous retention and triplet verification.
Error enlargement preserves super-regularity by
\noderef{SuperRegularPair} and \noderef{RegularPair}: for errors
$b\le c$, a rectangle larger than $c$ times each part is also larger
than $b$ times each part; its lower bound $d-b$ is at least $d-c$
and its upper bound $d+b$ is at most $d+c$. The degree windows enlarge
by the same inequalities. By the inner-reserve field of
\noderef{ColourRoundStepParameters}, $\xi_t<\zeta_t$, so enlarge
$W_{12}$ and each $F_{23}^j$ to error $\zeta_t$.

The parts $U,K$ have the same size $m=|Y|$, since their copy maps
are injective and source and host cluster sizes agree.
Each $|Z_j|$ is $n$ or $n+1$, as is $m$, giving exactly the three
near-equal alternatives. The source and host copies are disjoint.
Also $K\cap Z_j=\varnothing$, because $ij\in E(R)$ implies $i\ne j$,
whereas a vertex belonging to both host fibers would give $i=j$.
The projection $\mathcal J\to N_R(i)$ is injective; the degree clause
of \noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} therefore gives
$|\mathcal J|\le D$.

The density fields in \noderef{ColourRoundStepParameters} give
$2\zeta_t<\rho_{e_j}$ and $2\zeta_t<d/2$, and their upper counterparts
give $\rho_{e_j}<1$ and $d/2<1$. Indeed
$0\le2\zeta_t<1-\rho_{e_j}$ implies $\rho_{e_j}<1$, and likewise for
$d/2$. Moreover $\zeta_t\le2\zeta_t<1-d/2\le1$.
The degree-loss field implies $\zeta_t\le\theta_t$.
Define $F$ to be the \noderef{SimultaneousGoodEdgeGraph} of
$W_{12},F_{13}^j,F_{23}^j$, with constant test error $\zeta_t$ and
densities $\rho_{e_j},d/2$.
For the two finite-sum hypotheses of
\noderef{NearEqualVaryingGoodEdgeSuperRegularity}, use
\[
 \sum_{j\in\mathcal J}\sqrt{\zeta_t}
   =|\mathcal J|\sqrt{\zeta_t}\le D\sqrt{\zeta_t},\qquad
 \sum_{j\in\mathcal J}\zeta_t
   =|\mathcal J|\zeta_t\le D\zeta_t.
\]
The regularity-loss and degree-loss fields give respectively
$\zeta_t+\sum_j\sqrt{\zeta_t}\le\theta_t$ and
$\zeta_t+2\sum_j\zeta_t\le\theta_t$.
The square-root field gives $2\sqrt{\zeta_t}\le\theta_t$.
All size, disjointness, density, error and super-regularity premises
of that theorem have now been checked. Its conclusion proves the full
$(\theta_t,\rho_x)$ super-regularity of $F$ on $U,K$.
Every edge of $F$ is an edge of $W_{12}$, in either orientation, so
$F\subseteq W_{12}\subseteq A_f^i$.

Since $0\le\zeta_t\le1$, we have $\zeta_t\le\sqrt{\zeta_t}$, hence
$2\zeta_t\le\theta_t$. For a retained edge from $U$ to $K$, the
reverse orientation in the defining symmetrization of $F$ is excluded
by $U\cap K=\varnothing$. Thus its defining test gives, for every $j$,
the codegree window
$(\rho_{e_j}d/2\pm2\zeta_t)|Z_j|$, with the actual future part size.
Enlarge this window to error $\theta_t$, and enlarge the two test-pair
errors from $\zeta_t$ to $\theta_t$. Together with the checked part
sizes, disjointness and retained super-regularity, these are precisely
all clauses of \noderef{NearEqualRegularTriplet}.
The deterministic transport supplied earlier has no hypothesis about
the retained graph $F$ and remains valid for every bijection $\sigma$.
It is therefore exactly the final conclusion of the lemma.
\end{proof}
