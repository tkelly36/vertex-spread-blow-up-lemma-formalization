\begin{definition}
Let $G$ be a graph on a finite vertex set and let $U,W$ be disjoint vertex sets.  A perfect matching of $G[U,W]$ is a bijection $f:U\to W$ such that $u f(u)\in E(G)$ for every $u\in U$.  Thus its edges are exactly $\{u f(u):u\in U\}$; in particular it saturates both parts.
\end{definition}
