\begin{lemma}[Initial colour-round law]
Let $B$ be a finite blow-up instance with candidacy system $A$ and reduced
graph $R$ on $[r]$. Fix $q>0$, a colouring $c:[r]\to\{0,\ldots,q-1\}$,
sequences $\varepsilon,p:\mathbb N\to[0,\infty]$, $D,n\in\mathbb N$,
and $d,C\in[0,\infty]$. Assume $p_0=d$, $C/n\leq1$, every reduced
vertex has at most $D$ neighbours, and distinct reduced vertices that are
adjacent or have a common neighbour have different colours.
Suppose \noderef{CandidacySuperRegular} holds with parameters
$(\varepsilon_0,d)$. Then there is a law $\mu$ on
\noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding} with empty domain satisfying
\[
 \mathcal I(0,\varnothing;\varnothing,\mu)
\]
of \noderef{ColourRoundInvariant}, with parameters $(\varepsilon,p,D,C,n)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We first justify a total representative for the empty-domain embedding;
its values outside the empty domain impose no embedding constraints.
For each source vertex $x$, its source fibre at
$j=B.\mathrm{sourceCluster}(x)$ is nonempty. Capacity in
\noderef{BlowUpInstance} says that the corresponding host fibre has at
least this cardinality, and hence is nonempty. Choose a host vertex from
that fibre for each $x$, obtaining a total function $f_0:V_H\to V_G$.
If $V_H$ is empty this is instead the unique empty function, also when
$V_G$ is empty. Injectivity on the empty domain, edge preservation on that
domain, and candidacy compatibility there are vacuous. Thus $f_0$ defines
an element of \noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding} with domain
$\varnothing$. Put $\mu$ equal to its point mass.

The round is legal because $q>0$. The degree and colouring clauses are
the assumptions, and the touched-set clause is vacuous. No colour is
negative, so the domain selected by colour less than zero or membership
in the empty touched set is empty. To check \noderef{VertexSpreadOn},
the bound $C/n\leq1$ is assumed. For a positive number $s$ of prescribed
vertices there is no map from $\operatorname{Fin}(s)$ to the empty domain,
so there is no such cylinder to test. For $s=0$ the cylinder is the entire
function space, has probability one, and the bound is $(C/n)^0=1$.

For the only supported outcome $f_0$ and any cluster $j$, both the
exposure finset and the touched-neighbour finset are empty. Their
cardinalities are zero, so the bounds by $D$ and by one hold and the
required witness error is $\varepsilon_0$ with density $p_0=d$.
There are no used source or host vertices. By \noderef{UpdatedCandidacy},
the updated allowed relation at the empty domain is exactly $A.allowed$:
both exclusions and the universal embedded-neighbour condition are
vacuous. By \noderef{UpdatedCandidacyCluster}, the updated cluster graph
is therefore precisely the original candidacy graph of cluster $j$.

More explicitly, take $W_j$ to be the graph whose edges are the allowed
pairs between the left copy of $X_j$ and the right copy of $V_j$, with
all other vertices isolated. Its containment in the updated cluster
graph is equality. The graph used in \noderef{CandidacySuperRegular}
may retain allowed pairs for other clusters, but on the parts
$L_j=\operatorname{inl}(X_j)$ and $Q_j=\operatorname{inr}(V_j)$ its
edges are exactly those of $W_j$. Indeed an allowed pair respects
clusters by \noderef{CandidacySystem}; restricting its source to $X_j$
automatically restricts its host to $V_j$. Thus all degrees into these
parts and all edge counts between their subsets are identical.
Expanding \noderef{SuperRegularPair} and \noderef{RegularPair} transfers
the asserted $(\varepsilon_0,d)$-super-regularity to $W_j$ on these
exact parts. This supplies every witness clause, using the original
candidacy graphs and no thinning. All clauses of
\noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} are now verified.
\end{proof}
