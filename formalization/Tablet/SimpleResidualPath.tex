\begin{definition}[Simple residual source-to-sink path]
For a feasible integral flow from \noderef{FiniteIntegralFlow}, a simple
residual source-to-sink path consists of a positive integer $m$ and an
injective sequence $p:\{0,\ldots,m\}\to V$ with $p(0)=s$, $p(m)=t$,
and $f(p(i),p(i+1))<c(p(i),p(i+1))$ for $0\leq i<m$.
Thus no vertex or directed edge is selected twice.
\end{definition}
