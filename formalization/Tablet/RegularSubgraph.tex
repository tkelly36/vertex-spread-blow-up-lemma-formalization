\begin{lemma}[Regular subgraph]
For every $0<\varepsilon'<1$ and every $0<d<1$, there are $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon'$ and $n_0\in\mathbb N$ with the following property.  Let $G$ be a finite bipartite graph with bipartition $\{U,W\}$, where $|U|=|W|=n\geq n_0$.  If $(U,W)$ is lower $(\varepsilon,d+\varepsilon)$-regular in the sense of \noderef{LowerRegularPair}, then $G$ has a spanning subgraph $G'\subseteq G$ for which $(U,W)$ is $(\varepsilon',d)$-regular in the sense of \noderef{RegularPair}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $\eta=\varepsilon'_{\mathbb R}$ and $d_{\mathbb R}$ for the real
values of the prescribed parameters. They are positive and less than one,
since the original parameters lie strictly between zero and one. Apply
\noderef{RegularExtractionParameters} to $\eta$ to obtain
$h,\gamma,\alpha,\varepsilon,l,M,n_0$ with all the scalar conditions
in that statement. In particular $0<\varepsilon<\eta$, $1\leq n_0$,
$M\leq n_0$, $\varepsilon M<\gamma$,
\[
 2\alpha+(6\gamma+9\alpha+6/l)/h^2<h/4,\qquad
 2\,4^n\exp(-h^4n^2/8)<1\quad(n\geq n_0).
\]
Use the extended-nonnegative-real image of this $\varepsilon$ as the
existential witness. These choices depend only on $\varepsilon'$, hence
in particular only on the two prescribed parameters.

Fix an input graph and its bipartition with $n\geq n_0$. Then
$n>0$, $M\leq n$, and the disjoint cover gives $|V|=2n$.
For $A\subseteq U$, $B\subseteq W$ of size strictly greater than
$\varepsilon n$, the input \noderef{LowerRegularPair} condition gives
\[
 (d+\varepsilon)-\varepsilon\leq\operatorname{PairDensity}(G,A,B).
\]
The parameter $\varepsilon$ is finite, so the extended subtraction
cancels to $d$. Both tested sets are nonempty. By
\noderef{PairDensityReal}, their density is finite and its real value
is the usual rational edge density viewed in $\mathbb R$.
Consequently
\[
 d_G(A,B)\geq d_{\mathbb R}\quad
       (|A|,|B|>\varepsilon n).                         \tag{1}
\]
Real and extended-real cardinality thresholds agree here, since
all parameters and cardinalities are finite.

Since $l\leq M\leq n\leq |V|$ and $\alpha>0$, apply
Mathlib's \texttt{szemeredi\_regularity} from
\texttt{Mathlib.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.Regularity.Lemma} on the entire vertex set.
This gives an equipartition $\mathcal P$ with
$l\leq m=|\mathcal P|\leq
\operatorname{SzemerediRegularity.bound}(\alpha,l)\leq M$ and
$\mathcal P$ $\alpha$-uniform. Apply
\noderef{RegularExtractionPartitionGeometry} with this same partition,
the disjoint cover, and (1). Its scalar hypotheses hold by the
parameter choice, including $\alpha\leq\gamma$ and
$\alpha\leq\gamma^2$. It yields $n/m\leq|S|\leq3n/m$ for every
part and the eligible ordered pair set $E$. For each $(S,T)\in E$,
the chunk density $D_{ST}=d_G(U\cap S,W\cap T)$ belongs to
$[d_{\mathbb R},1]$, and its restriction to intersections larger
than their $\gamma$ thresholds changes by less than $2\alpha$.
The total ineligible area is at most
$(4\gamma+9\alpha+6/l)n^2$. This statement explicitly includes
nonsubstantial chunks, ordered distinct nonuniform pairs, and
diagonal rectangles.

Apply \noderef{WeightedRectangleExpectation} to $\mathcal P,E$ and
these local bounds.
It gives a function $p:V\times V\to[0,1]$, zero on nonedges and
outside $U\times W$. For an edge in an eligible rectangle it is
$d_{\mathbb R}/D_{ST}$, a valid probability because
$0<d_{\mathbb R}\leq D_{ST}$; in every other rectangle it is one.
The unique parts of each endpoint determine the rectangle.
The function is used only in the canonical $U$-to-$W$ orientation.
Put
\[
 \mu(A,B)=\sum_{(a,b)\in A\times B}p(a,b).
\]
The weighted-expectation statement pays both the ineligible area
and the additional small-intersection strips. Specialize its final
conditional conclusion to the chosen $h>0$ and its scalar margin to get
\[
 |\mu(A,B)-d_{\mathbb R}|A||B||<(h/4)|A||B|
        \quad(|A|,|B|>hn).                             \tag{2}
\]

The cutoff supplied by the parameters is precisely the strict
hypothesis of \noderef{SimultaneousUnequalRetention}. Apply that
node to $G,U,W,n,h,p$. It gives a graph $G'$ on the same entire
vertex set, with decidable adjacency and $G'\leq G$, retaining
the weight-one edges, for which all large tests simultaneously obey
\[
 |e_{G'}(A,B)-\mu(A,B)|\leq(h/4)|A||B|
        \quad(|A|,|B|>hn).                             \tag{3}
\]
Its finite product law has one trial for each canonically oriented
undirected edge; the probabilities were validated before forming
that law. The simultaneous conclusion uses all subset tests, not
only tests consisting of partition parts.

Now let $A\subseteq U$, $B\subseteq W$ be any of the tests in
\noderef{RegularPair} for $\varepsilon'$. Their real sizes satisfy
$|A|,|B|>\eta n>hn$, because $h<\eta$ and $n>0$.
Combining (2) and (3) by the triangle inequality gives
\[
 |e_{G'}(A,B)-d_{\mathbb R}|A||B||<(h/2)|A||B|.
\]
The two cardinalities are positive, so divide by their product.
By \noderef{PairDensityReal} this gives
$|d_{G'}(A,B)-d_{\mathbb R}|<h/2<\eta$.
In particular the real density belongs to
$[d_{\mathbb R}-\eta,d_{\mathbb R}+\eta]$.

All tested densities are finite by \noderef{PairDensityReal}.
Thus the upper real inequality converts to
$\operatorname{PairDensity}(G',A,B)\leq d+\varepsilon'$.
If $\varepsilon'\leq d$, finite extended subtraction has real value
$d_{\mathbb R}-\eta$, so the lower real inequality converts to
$d-\varepsilon'\leq\operatorname{PairDensity}(G',A,B)$.
If instead $d\leq\varepsilon'$, the truncated lower endpoint is zero
and the same conclusion follows from nonnegativity.
These are every required strict-threshold test of
\noderef{RegularPair}. The graph has the original vertex type,
including isolated vertices, and is contained in $G$, so it is the
required spanning subgraph. No minimum-degree hypothesis was used.
\end{proof}
