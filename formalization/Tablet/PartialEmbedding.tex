\begin{lemma}[Partial embedding lemma]
For every $\Delta\in\mathbb N$ and $0<d\leq1$, there is $0<d'_0<d$ such that, for every $0<d'\leq d'_0$, there is a finite real $\varepsilon_0>0$ such that, for every $0<\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon_0$, there is a finite real $C_0\geq1$ such that, for every finite real $C\geq C_0$, there is $n_0\in\mathbb N$ with the following property for every integer $n\geq n_0$. All scalar parameters in this hierarchy are finite real numbers.

Let $(H,G,R,(X_i)_{i\in[r]},(V_i)_{i\in[r]},(A^i)_{i \in [r]})$ be a finite blow-up instance with candidacy graphs.  Suppose it is host-lower-$(\varepsilon,d)$-regular in the sense of \noderef{InstanceLowerRegular}, $\Delta(H),\Delta(R)\leq\Delta$, $|X_i|\leq\varepsilon|V_i|$, and $(1-\varepsilon)n\leq|V_i|\leq(1+\varepsilon)n$ for every $i$.  Suppose also that every $x\in X_i$ has at least $dn$ candidates in $A^i$.

Then there is a probability distribution on compatible embeddings $\varphi:H\hookrightarrow G$ whose underlying maps are $(C/n)$-vertex-spread on the whole codomain $V(G)$ in the sense of \noderef{VertexSpreadOn}.  Moreover, for every $X\subseteq V(H)$ there is such a $(C/n)$-vertex-spread distribution on partial compatible embeddings $f:H[X]\hookrightarrow G$ for which every unembedded vertex $x$ has at least $d'n$ candidates in its restricted updated candidacy graph $A_f^i$ from \noderef{UpdatedCandidacyCluster}, where $x\in X_i$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{PartialEmbeddingParameters} to $\Delta,d$ and use its
$d'_0,\varepsilon_0,C_0,n_0$ in precisely the quantifier order in the
statement. Fix the subsequent parameters, $n\geq n_0$, and an instance
satisfying the hypotheses. Put $a=d(d/2)^\Delta$ and $q=C/n$.
The parameter lemma gives $n>0$, $q\leq1$, $0<a\leq d$,
$\varepsilon\leq1/2$, the power floor
$a\leq d(d-\varepsilon)^k$ for $k\leq\Delta$, and
\[
 \varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)<a,\quad
 (\Delta+1)\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)\leq a/2,\quad d'\leq a/4.
\]
Since $\Delta+1\geq1$, the loss bound also gives
$\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)\leq a/2$, and $d'\leq a/2$.
All parameter choices are independent of $r$ and the graphs.

For a total map $f:V(H)\to V(G)$ define $\mathrm{Good}(S,f)$ to mean
that $f$ is the underlying map of a greedy state on $S$ from
\noderef{GreedyPartialEmbedding}. We check the three invariant
hypotheses of the finite law construction explicitly.
First, goodness depends only on values on $S$. Indeed, injectivity
on $S$, edge preservation inside $S$, and compatibility on $S$ use
only those values by \noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding}.
For an unembedded $x$, the raw set from \noderef{RawCandidates}
uses only images of vertices in $S$. Its cardinality is consequently
unchanged when the map is changed outside $S$, as is the exposure
count $|S\cap N_H(x)|$. Thus the raw-bound field of the greedy state
is unchanged too. This proves both directions of the locality
equivalence by transporting the state to the new total map.

Second, every total map is good on the empty set. The three partial
compatibility requirements are vacuous. The raw set is the original
candidate set, its exposure count is zero, and its raw bound is
exactly the assumed inequality $dn\leq|\{v:A(x,v)\}|$.

Third, let $z\notin S$ and let $f$ be good on $S$.
Apply \noderef{GreedyPartialEmbeddingStep} to a witnessing state.
Its hypotheses are the parameter inequalities above, host lower
regularity, the degree bound on $H$, source-cluster smallness, and
the upper host-size bound. It gives a nonempty legal set $L$ of
size at least $(a/2)n$, and for every $v\in L$ a good state on
$S\cup\{z\}$ with total map exactly $f[z\mapsto v]$.
The reciprocal-cardinality clause of
\noderef{PartialEmbeddingParameters} gives $1/|L|\leq q$.
In particular this step bounds bad rows by lower regularity and
removes occupied images within the single source cluster; it does
not introduce any loss depending on $|S|$ or $r$.

For each source vertex $u$, take the fallback set $T(u)=V_{c(u)}$.
The lower host-size hypothesis gives
$|T(u)|\geq(1-\varepsilon)n$. The other reciprocal-cardinality
clause of \noderef{PartialEmbeddingParameters} shows that $T(u)$
is nonempty and $1/|T(u)|\leq q$. We have now checked every
hypothesis of \noderef{FiniteGreedySpreadLaw}. For every prescribed
$X$ it supplies a PMF on total maps good on $X$ whose projected law
is $q$-vertex-spread on all of $V(G)$. Its proof uses the checked
uniform probabilities of Mathlib's \texttt{PMF.toOuterMeasure\_uniformOfFinset\_apply} and
the full joint-cylinder result of \noderef{VertexSpreadSequential}:
kernels have a spread bound even at zero-mass invalid prefixes, and
stored values outside $X$ are appended independently and uniformly
from $T(u)$, rather than deterministically fixed.

For each good map in this law choose a witnessing greedy state.
By \noderef{GreedyPartialEmbeddingReserve}, its underlying partial
compatible embedding has at least $d'n$ updated candidates for
every $x\notin X$. The hypotheses of that reserve lemma are the
power floor, $\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)\leq a/2$, $d'\leq a/2$,
the source degree bound, cluster smallness, and the upper host-size
bound, all established above. Moreover, completing stored values
outside $X$ cannot change the updated graph by
\noderef{UpdatedCandidacyCongruence}, since the maps agree on $X$.
Every candidate for $x$ lies in $V_{c(x)}$ by
\noderef{CandidacySystem}, so the restriction in
\noderef{UpdatedCandidacyCluster} removes no additional candidate
for this $x$.

Push the law forward to the subtype of partial compatible embeddings
with these reserve proofs. Choosing the witness and adding the
proofs does not change its underlying total map: by the definition
of goodness, that map is exactly the original good map. The
projection of the pushforward back to total maps is therefore the
original projected law. Every cylinder probability is identical,
giving the partial conclusion with full joint spread on total maps.

For the full conclusion apply the same finite-law construction with
$X=V(H)$. Every witnessing state is injective on the whole source,
preserves every source edge, and satisfies $A(x,f(x))$ for every $x$.
It therefore gives a graph copy which is compatible in the sense of
\noderef{CompatibleEmbedding}. Push forward to these compatible
copies. Once again the underlying total maps are unchanged, so
their projected law has the same cylinders and the same spread bound.

If the source is empty, the construction uses the unique empty map
and no fallback fiber, even if the host is empty. If $X$ is empty
but the source is not, only the independent completion is performed,
and the initial degree bound gives the reserve. When $\Delta=0$,
the exposure count is always zero and $a=d$; the parameter and
extension lemmas use $\Delta+1$, not division by $\Delta$, and
remain valid. Thus all stated endpoint cases are included.
\end{proof}
