\begin{lemma}[One-colour-class matching extension]
Let \(R\) have maximum degree at most \(D\), let \(c:V(R)\to[q]\)
distinguish vertices at distance at most two, and fix \(t<q\).
Suppose a particular law \(\mu\) satisfies
\(\mathcal I(t,S;X(t,S),\mu)\) of \noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} for
\(S\subseteq c^{-1}(t)\), and let \(i\in c^{-1}(t)\setminus S\).
Write \(x_j\) for the number of already embedded neighbours of an
unembedded cluster \(j\), and assume
\(\varepsilon_t\leq\varepsilon_{t+1}\leq1\).

The certificate has certified round bound $q$; hence the hypothesis $t<q$
licenses each of its guarantee clauses used below.  Assume every source and host cluster has the same cardinality in
\(\{n,n+1\}\), the initial host pairs are
\((\varepsilon_0,d)\)-super-regular, and every reduced edge carries a
saturating source matching. Fix an \noderef{InductionApplicationCertificate}
whose thinning, matching-existence, matching-spread, and Four-Graphs
conclusions use the displayed parameter sequences.

Assume that every \((\varepsilon_t,D_x)\)-super-regular witness and relevant
host pair on parts of sizes in \(\{n,n+1\}\) admits the required contained
spanning thinning, and that
\[
 2\sqrt{\zeta_t}\leq\theta_t,\qquad
 \zeta_t+D\sqrt{\zeta_t}\leq\theta_t,\qquad
 \zeta_t+2D\zeta_t\leq\theta_t,
\]
and, for every exposure \(x\leq D\),
\[
 0<\theta_t,\quad 4\theta_t<\rho_x,\quad
 2\zeta_t<\rho_x,\quad 2\zeta_t<1-\rho_x,\quad
 2\zeta_t<d/2,\quad 2\zeta_t<1-d/2.
\]
Assume also that, for every \(x<D\),
\[
 D_{x+1}=\rho_xd/2,
 \qquad D_{x+1}+\varepsilon_t+1/n\leq D_x.
\]
where \(\theta_t\) lies below the Four-Graphs input cutoff for all ordered
density triples \((\rho_x,\rho_y,d/2)\), \(x,y\leq D\), with output error
\(\omega_{t+1}<\varepsilon_{t+1}\). Take \(n\) above the finitely many
thinning, matching, and Four-Graphs thresholds. Suppose each retained current
pair has a uniform \((C/(2n))\)-vertex-spread matching law, each relevant
Four-Graphs failure probability is at most \((1-a)^n\), and
\[
 D(1-a)^n\leq\tfrac12,\qquad C/n\leq1.
\]
More precisely, for every exposure at most \(D\), all four certificate
thresholds are at most \(n\), its matching constant is at most \(C/2\), and
for every ordered pair \(x,y\leq D\) its Four-Graphs threshold is at most \(n\) and
its exponential constant is at least \(a\), where \(0<a\leq1\). For the
present round assume
\[
 n^{-1}\leq\xi_t<\zeta_t,\quad \xi_t+2/n\leq\zeta_t,\quad
 \xi_t+(\zeta_tn)^{-1}\leq\zeta_t,
\]
\[
 \omega_{t+1}+2/n\leq\varepsilon_{t+1},\qquad
 \omega_{t+1}+(\varepsilon_{t+1}n)^{-1}\leq\varepsilon_{t+1}.
\]
Then there is a total conditional extension kernel \(\kappa\), assigning to
every partial embedding of \(X(t,S)\) a law on compatible partial embeddings
of \(X(t,S\cup\{i\})\), such that, for every input embedding
\(f\in\operatorname{supp}(\mu)\), every extension in
\(\operatorname{supp}(\kappa(f))\) agrees with \(f\) on \(X(t,S)\), and the
restriction of \(\kappa(f)\) to the newly embedded cluster is
\((C/n)\)-vertex-spread. The
output law is exactly the composition
\(\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{bind}}\kappa\).  This very law satisfies
\(\mathcal I(t,S\cup\{i\};X(t,S\cup\{i\}),\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{bind}}\kappa)\).
Every future
neighbour \(j\) of \(i\) acquires a contained
\((\varepsilon_{t+1},D_{x_j+1})\)-super-regular witness in its true updated
candidacy graph. A witness touched before processing \(i\) remains a contained
\((\varepsilon_{t+1},D_{x_j})\)-super-regular witness, while every witness
which is neither previously nor newly touched retains its
\((\varepsilon_t,D_{x_j})\)-super-regular witness.  The current cluster and
every future witness tested at it are untouched, and hence have input error
\(\varepsilon_t\).  In every case the new law remains
\((C/n)\)-vertex-spread and the touched-neighbour set has size at most one.

\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Package the displayed certificate and numerical inequalities as the data
record of \noderef{ColourRoundStepParameters}; every field is one of the
hypotheses, with exactly the original sequences and the same certified round
bound \(q\). The signature and all parameter dependencies are unchanged.

Fix \(f\in\operatorname{supp}(\mu)\). By
\noderef{ColourRoundUntouchedGeometry}, the current cluster and every
future neighbour tested at it have no neighbour in \(S\). Their stored
witnesses therefore have the common error \(\varepsilon_t\); the exposure
indices \(x_i,x_j\) remain independent, with \(x_i\le D\) and \(x_j<D\).
The latter lemma also gives the exact exposure increment and the at-most-one
touched-neighbour bound after adding \(i\).

Apply \noderef{ColourRoundMatchingPreparation} to this supported \(f\),
the original host super-regularity, the saturating source pairs, and the
numerical record. It uses the actual witness and host thinning guarantees,
the simultaneous retained-edge estimate, and the ordered Four-Graphs
guarantees, explicitly at \(t<q\) and the two separate exposure bounds.
The density upper bounds used there follow from
\(2\zeta_t<1-\rho_x\) and \(2\zeta_t<1-d/2\).
Its transport components are
\noderef{EqualSizeSourceWitnessTransport},
\noderef{CurrentExtraSourceWitnessTransport}, and
\noderef{FutureExtraSourceWitnessTransport}.
They keep the parts of sizes \(n\) or \(n+1\) and the codegree windows
normalised by the actual future part.
In the current-extra case the source map has only the residual domain:
the artificial row is inserted before testing and deleted after Four
Graphs. In the future-extra case the unmatched future row is deleted
before testing; the output is transported to the exact residual source
part, and a subset of the genuine unchanged old row is restored.
Each transport uses both exact-image equivalences and proves containment
in the true updated candidacy graph, not equality with that graph.

The preparation supplies a local raw law \(\nu_0\), an event \(E\) with
probability at least one half and a supported member, and the conditioned
law \(\nu_f=\nu_0(\,\cdot\mid E)\).
Its restriction to the new cluster is \(C/n\)-vertex-spread, and every
supported local embedding has the required contained witness for each
future neighbour, for every compatible lift agreeing with \(f\) on the
old domain. The conditioning concerns this one current-cluster matching
only, not a global event for the colour class.

These are precisely the pointwise input hypotheses of
\noderef{ColourRoundKernelAssembly}. That lemma lifts through
\noderef{ExtendUpdatedPartialEmbedding}, uses a supported input's law
as filler outside the old support to produce a total kernel, and checks
all the invariant clauses. Non-neighbours keep their old true candidacy
sets and witnesses by \noderef{UpdatedCandidacyNonneighborExtension};
the touched and untouched errors are preserved separately.
The conclusion of \noderef{ColourRoundKernelAssembly} includes the
full joint spread clause for the very law
\(\mu\mathbin{\mathrm{bind}}\kappa\), as part of its asserted
colour-round invariant.
Thus the kernel has both asserted support properties and its exact bind
law satisfies the new colour-round invariant, as required.
\end{proof}
