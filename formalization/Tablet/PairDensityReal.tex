\begin{lemma}[Real density adapter]
For a simple graph $G$ and nonempty finite vertex sets $A,B$, the value
$\operatorname{PairDensity}(G,A,B)$ of \noderef{PairDensity} is finite.
Its real value equals Mathlib's rational edge density, viewed in $\mathbb R$,
and both equal
\[
 \frac{|\{(a,b)\in A\times B:G(a,b)\}|}{|A||B|}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Nonemptiness makes both cardinalities positive, so their product is
nonzero. The finite numerator divided by this nonzero finite denominator
is finite. Applying the real-value map to the quotient in
\noderef{PairDensity} gives the displayed real quotient. Expanding
Mathlib's edge-density definition gives the same filtered Cartesian
product and the same denominator, first in $\mathbb Q$ and then in
$\mathbb R$. Thus the two densities agree.
\end{proof}
