\begin{lemma}[All cuts of the factor network]
For the network $N$ of \noderef{BipartiteFactorNetwork},
$\sum_v c(s,v)=k|U|$. Every source-side cut $S$ has sets $A\subseteq U$
and $B\subseteq W$ such that
\[
 C(S)=k|A|+e_G(U\setminus A,W\setminus B)+k|B|.
\]
Here $e_G(X,Y)$ counts ordered pairs $(x,y)\in X\times Y$ with $G$-adjacency;
the formula does not require equal part sizes or disjoint ambient sets.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the exact copies from \noderef{BipartiteFactorNetwork}. Define $A$ to
be the ambient elements of $U$ whose left copies are outside $S$, and
$B$ to be the ambient elements of $W$ whose right copies are inside $S$.
The copy maps are injective and onto their corresponding parts, so
$A\subseteq U$, $B\subseteq W$, and
$S=\{s\}\sqcup(U\setminus A)\sqcup B$, with the copied parts understood.
The source is in $S$ and the sink is outside $S$ by the cut hypotheses.

The only positive capacities, by the network definition, are source-to-left,
left-to-right, and right-to-sink. A source-to-left pair crosses exactly
when its left endpoint lies in $A$, giving $k|A|$. A left-to-right pair
crosses exactly when its endpoints are in $U\setminus A$ and
$W\setminus B$, and has capacity one exactly for a $G$ edge. Because
the subtype copies are exact and injective, these pairs are in bijection
with the ordered edges counted by the displayed middle term; no edge
is duplicated. A right-to-sink pair crosses exactly for a right endpoint
in $B$, giving $k|B|$. All other pairs have capacity zero. Summing these
three disjoint kinds of pairs proves the formula. Finally, exactly the
$|U|$ source-to-left pairs contribute to $\sum_v c(s,v)$, each with
capacity $k$, giving its asserted value. Empty parts and $k=0$ require
no exception to these finite sums.
\end{proof}
