\begin{lemma}[Counting ordered two-edge paths]
Let $U\subseteq A$ and $W\subseteq B$ be finite sets and let $P$ be a relation
between $A$ and $B$. Then
\[
 \sum_{u\in U}|\{w\in W:P(u,w)\}|^2
 =\sum_{(w,z)\in W^2}|\{u\in U:P(u,w)\text{ and }P(u,z)\}|.
\]
The ordered pairs on the right include pairs with $w=z$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Both sides count triples $(u,w,z)\in U\times W\times W$ for which
$P(u,w)$ and $P(u,z)$ hold. For a fixed $u$, the two endpoints can be
chosen independently from its related elements of $W$, giving the square
on the left; no distinctness condition is imposed. For a fixed ordered
pair $(w,z)$, the allowed middle elements are exactly the elements counted
on the right. Equivalently, expand both sides as the finite sum of
$\mathbf1_{P(u,w)}\mathbf1_{P(u,z)}$ over all such triples and interchange
the three finite summations. This proves the identity, including the
diagonal terms.
\end{proof}
