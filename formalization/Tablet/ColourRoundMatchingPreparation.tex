\begin{lemma}[Retained triplets and local success law]
Fix a blow-up instance $B$, candidacy system $A$, square colouring $c$,
sequences $\theta,p,\varepsilon,\rho,\zeta,\omega,\xi$, and a
\noderef{ColourRoundStepParameters} record at $(D,q,d,C,a,n,t)$.
Assume each source cluster has size $n$ or $n+1$ and its host cluster
has the same size, the initial host pairs are
$(\varepsilon_0,d)$-super-regular, and every reduced edge is a
\noderef{SaturatingSourcePair}. Suppose
\(\mathcal I(t,S;X,\mu)\) of \noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} holds,
$i\in c^{-1}(t)\setminus S$, and $f\in\operatorname{supp}(\mu)$.
Put $Y=X_i$, and let $X'$ be the source domain indexed by the earlier
colour classes and $S\cup\{i\}$.
There exist a law $\nu_0$ on compatible partial embeddings of $Y$ for
the updated system $A_f$, an event $E$ in that record space, and a
supported member of $E$, such that
\[
\nu_0(E)\ge1/2,\quad
(\nu_0|_Y)\text{ is }C/(2n)\text{-vertex-spread},\quad
(\nu_0(\,\cdot\mid E)|_Y)\text{ is }C/n\text{-vertex-spread}.
\]
For every supported $g$ of this conditioned law, every compatible
embedding $h$ on $X'$ agreeing with $f$ on $X$ and $g$ on $Y$, and every
unembedded neighbour $j$ of $i$ (that is, $j\notin S$ and $c(j)\ge t$),
the true updated candidacy graph of $j$ at $h$ contains a spanning
$(\varepsilon_{t+1},p_{e_j+1})$-super-regular witness. Here
$e_j=|N_R(j)\cap(\{k:c(k)<t\}\cup S)|$, and the parts are exactly
the source vertices of $j$ outside $X'$ and the host vertices of $j$
outside $h(X')$, not enlarged or abstract image parts.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We use \noderef{ColourRoundRetainedTriplets} for the deterministic
retained graph and its family of triplets, and
\noderef{ColourRoundMatchingRecords} for matching records and containment
in the true updated graphs. The following paragraphs record the
parameter verifications and then compose their conclusions with the
local matching law.
Write $\mathcal C$ for the certificate in the parameter record. By
\noderef{ColourRoundUntouchedGeometry}, $i$ and every neighbour tested
in this lemma have no neighbour in $S$. The witness clause of
\noderef{ColourRoundInvariant} therefore gives their common input
error $\varepsilon_t$. Their exposure counts $x=e_i$ and $y=e_j$
are bounded by $D$, and $y<D$. We make no comparison between $x$ and
$y$. At every use of a certificate guarantee below, give it $t<q$
and separately the bound on each exposure argument.

An unembedded cluster has no source vertices in $X$. Its host cluster
contains no vertex of $f(X)$: the cluster-respecting clause of
\noderef{CandidacySystem} and compatibility in
\noderef{PartialCompatibleEmbedding} put every image in its processed
source cluster. Thus the witness parts are the full clusters, of their
actual sizes $n$ or $n+1$. The inequalities
$2\zeta_t<1-\rho_x$ and $2\zeta_t<1-d/2$ imply respectively
$\rho_x<1$ and $d/2<1$; these conclusions do not follow merely from
super-regularity. Together with the lower density inequalities they
give $0<\rho_x,d/2<1$ and $\zeta_t<1$.
Consequently $\xi_t$ is finite, and $1/n\le\xi_t$ implies $n\ge1$.
Also $\varepsilon_t\le\varepsilon_{t+1}\le1$.

For the current witness and each future witness apply
$\mathcal C.\mathrm{witnessThinningGuarantee}$ at $(t,x)$ and $(t,y)$.
Disjointness comes from the source/host copies. Both part sizes are at
least $n$, and differ by at most one, so the scalar threshold bounds
license both sides of every thinning. This produces contained
$(\xi_t,\rho_x)$ and $(\xi_t,\rho_y)$ pairs.
For each relevant host pair apply
$\mathcal C.\mathrm{hostThinningGuarantee}$ at $t$, to the original
$(\varepsilon_0,d)$ host pair from \noderef{InstanceSuperRegular}.
Its threshold is also at most both actual sizes. The result is a
contained $(\xi_t,d/2)$ pair.
All these are the actual thinning guarantees from
\noderef{InductionApplicationCertificate}.

Let $m=|X_i|=|V_i|$ and $k_j=|X_j|=|V_j|$.
View all the pairs on the finite ambient sum of source and host vertices.
The canonical injections preserve each part cardinality, each degree
and each rectangle edge count, so this change of ambient type preserves
all the displayed pair parameters.
The source-pair definition gives an injection in one of the two
orientations whose edges are exactly the source edges. If the sizes are
equal, the injection is bijective and either orientation yields a
bijection from the current to the future source part. If the current
part is larger, an injection from it to the smaller part is impossible,
so the other orientation is provided; its image misses exactly one
current vertex. If the future part is larger, the injection is from
the current part and misses exactly one future vertex. These statements
follow by counting the image of the injection and the one-element
complement, and use only \noderef{SaturatingSourcePair}.

For each $j$ apply the corresponding preparation-and-transport result:
\noderef{EqualSizeSourceWitnessTransport} if $m=k_j$,
\noderef{CurrentExtraSourceWitnessTransport} if $m=k_j+1$, and
\noderef{FutureExtraSourceWitnessTransport} if $k_j=m+1$.
Take $O$ to be the stored old witness, $W$ its thinning, $J$ the
thinned host pair, and $H$ the original host pair. In the unequal
cases the size parameter is $n$, because the smaller size is $n$.
The inner reserves are exactly those in the parameter record; its
integer reserve supplies the artificial-row rounding bound. The outer
reserves are applied at $y\le D$, with
$\omega=\omega_{t+1}$ and $e=\varepsilon_{t+1}$.
The density $\rho_y d/2$ is at most one because each factor is at
most one. In the future-extra case the old error is at most one and
the density gap is
$\rho_y d/2+\varepsilon_t+1/n\le p_y$, using the density-update
identity at $y<D$. Thus every hypothesis of all three cases is verified.
They give graphs $F_{13}^j$ on the full current source part and the full
future host part, of parameters $(\zeta_t,\rho_y)$, and a deterministic
contained-witness transport for every successful matching. In particular
the future-extra row is deleted before testing, and the current-extra
artificial row is inserted before testing. No total current-to-future
source map is used in the latter case.

Weaken the current and host thinning errors to $\zeta_t$; error
enlargement only increases degree windows and reduces the rectangles
tested in \noderef{SuperRegularPair}.
Index the tests by the unembedded neighbours of $i$, a finite set of
cardinality at most $D$. Form the graph of retained current edges with
the common-neighbour window
\[
(\rho_y d/2-2\zeta_t)k_j\ \le
 |N_{F_{13}^j}(u)\cap N_{F_{23}^j}(v)\cap V_j|
 \le (\rho_y d/2+2\zeta_t)k_j.
\]
This is exactly \noderef{SimultaneousGoodEdgeGraph} with constant
error family $\zeta_t$. Apply
\noderef{NearEqualVaryingGoodEdgeSuperRegularity}.
Its density lower hypotheses follow from $2\zeta_t<\rho_y,d/2$,
and its upper hypotheses from the strict complementary inequalities
already used. Its square-root premise and the two finite-sum premises
follow from the three displayed loss bounds in the parameter record:
$\sum_j\sqrt{\zeta_t}\le D\sqrt{\zeta_t}$ and
$\sum_j\zeta_t\le D\zeta_t$.
The base error satisfies $\zeta_t\le\theta_t$ by the regularity-loss
bound. Thus the retained current pair is
$(\theta_t,\rho_x)$-super-regular. Moreover
$2\zeta_t\le2\sqrt{\zeta_t}\le\theta_t$ because
$0\le\zeta_t\le1$. The retained window therefore implies the
$(\rho_y d/2\pm\theta_t)k_j$ window. All three pairs, their actual
size relations, their disjointness, and this window give the precise
\noderef{NearEqualRegularTriplet} at $(\theta_t,\rho_x,\rho_y,d/2)$.

Use $\mathcal C.\mathrm{matchingExistenceGuarantee}$ at $(t,x)$,
size $m\ge n$, on the retained pair. Its input error is $\theta_t$,
not $\theta_t/2$. Use
$\mathcal C.\mathrm{matchingGuarantee}$ at the same round and
exposure. The uniformly chosen retained matching has ambient-value
spread at most
$\mathcal C.\mathrm{matchingConstant}(t,x)/m\le C/(2m)$.
The passage between its host subtype and ambient host values is
injective; a prescribed value outside the host part has probability
zero, and values inside give the identical cylinder. Hence the same
bound holds for matching functions into the host subtype.
These certificate fields can be used at their own vertex-type universes.
Relabel the finite ambient type to a lifted finite ordinal, using
\noderef{FiniteTripletRelabelling}. It preserves part sizes,
super-regularity, triplets, matching bijections, and matching-induced
success conditions. The matching bijection pushes the uniform law to
the uniform law: each matching has mass the reciprocal of the same
finite matching count. Pulling back cylinders through the part
bijections gives identical cylinders. Thus the certificate bounds apply
to the original retained graph, even if the field and ambient universes
differ; no threshold is changed.
For every test use
$\mathcal C.\mathrm{fourGraphsGuarantee}$ at $(t,x,y)$, explicitly
with $x,y\le D$ and $t<q$. Its threshold is at most $n\le m$ and
the triplet has just been verified. It gives failure at most
$(1-a_{t,x,y})^m\le(1-a)^m$, since $a\le a_{t,x,y}\le1$.

Apply \noderef{OneClusterExtension} at actual size $m$, with constant
$C_m=Cm/n$, so $C_m/m=C/n$ and $C_m/(2m)=C/(2n)$.
The raw spread bound $C/(2m)\le C/(2n)$ supplies its spread premise;
the scalar budget is
$D(1-a)^m\le D(1-a)^n\le1/2$.
Its density hypotheses are $0<\theta_t$, $4\theta_t<\rho_x\le1$;
its spread-unit hypothesis is the recorded $C/n\le1$.
All its conclusions therefore hold for this one current matching:
the intersection of the local success events has probability at least
one half, contains a supported matching, and its conditioned law is
$C/n$-spread. For the preliminary law use the matching-spread guarantee
and $m\ge n$ to get the weaker $C/(2n)$ bound. These enlargements are
justified by \noderef{VertexSpreadMonotone}.

Every retained matching is a compatible partial embedding of $Y$ for
the updated system: it is injective, its edges lie in the current
contained witness, and $Y$ is independent by \noderef{BlowUpInstance}.
The injection $\ell$ in \noderef{ColourRoundMatchingRecords} chooses
fixed values off $Y$, and on $Y$ evaluates the matching via a source
bijection $e:Y\to U$ and host injection $v:K\to V(G)$.
Let $\lambda$ be the uniform law on perfect matching records and
$\lambda_f$ its pushforward to matching functions. If $E_f$ is the
simultaneous-success event supplied by \noderef{OneClusterExtension},
put $E_0=\{\sigma:\sigma\in E_f\}$ in matching-record space,
$\nu_0=\ell_*\lambda$, and $E=\ell(E_0)$.
Injectivity gives $\ell^{-1}(E)=E_0$, so
\[
 \nu_0(E)=\lambda(E_0)=\lambda_f(E_f)\ge1/2.
\]
A supported successful function has a supported matching-record
preimage, and its image under $\ell$ is the required supported record.
For any pushforward $b_*\lambda$ and any event $S$ of positive mass,
the filter formula is
\[
 \bigl(b_*(\lambda\mid b^{-1}S)\bigr)(z)
 =\frac{\sum_{\sigma:b(\sigma)=z}1_S(b(\sigma))\lambda(\sigma)}
        {\lambda(b^{-1}S)}
 =\bigl((b_*\lambda)\mid S\bigr)(z).
\]
Use this with $b=\ell,S=E$, and with the matching-record forgetful map
and $S=E_f$. The two evaluation pushforwards therefore commute with
conditioning and have exactly the unconditioned and conditioned
matching-function laws transported by
$\sigma\mapsto(y\mapsto v(\sigma(e(y))))$.
For an injective family of source targets $y_b\in Y$ and host targets
$w_b$, if some $w_b$ is outside $v(K)$, its cylinder has empty preimage
and probability zero. Otherwise choose the unique $k_b\in K$ with
$v(k_b)=w_b$. Its preimage is exactly the matching cylinder
$\sigma(e(y_b))=k_b$ for all $b$. The indices $e(y_b)$ remain
distinct, so both matching spread estimates transfer unchanged.
This proves the raw $C/(2n)$ and conditioned $C/n$ estimates also
for cylinders whose ambient host targets need not lie in the host part.

Finally fix a supported successful record. It has a matching-record
preimage $\sigma$, and membership in $E$ and injectivity of $\ell$
give $\sigma\in E_0$. Thus all the success clauses of $E_f$ hold.
For each tested neighbour, \noderef{ColourRoundRetainedTriplets}
supplies $W\subseteq T^j_\sigma$ with the required future
super-regularity. For any compatible agreeing lift $h$,
\noderef{ColourRoundMatchingRecords} gives
$T^j_\sigma\subseteq A_h^j$ and both exact unused-part equalities.
Compose these containments; no equality with the true candidacy graph
is asserted. The future exposure is less than $D$ by
\noderef{ColourRoundUntouchedGeometry}, so the recorded identity
$\rho_y d/2=p_{y+1}$ supplies the required density.
The error is $\varepsilon_{t+1}$, and the argument holds for every
compatible agreeing lift, completing the pointwise conclusion.
\end{proof}
