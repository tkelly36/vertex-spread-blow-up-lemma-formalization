\begin{lemma}[Uniform extraction tail cutoffs]
For real $c,h>0$ and an integer $M\geq0$, there is an integer
$n_0\geq\max\{1,M\}$ such that, for every integer $n\geq n_0$,
$hn\geq1$ and each of
\[
4n e^{-c^2n/8},\qquad 2\,4^n e^{-h^4n^2/8},\qquad
4n e^{-2h^2n},\qquad 2\,4^n e^{-2h^4n^2}
\]
is strictly less than $1/4$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All four expressions are positive for $n\geq1$. For $a>0$,
$\log(4n)-an$ tends to $-\infty$: indeed
$\log n/n\to0$, so eventually $\log n\leq an/2$, and then
$\log(4n)-an\leq\log4-an/2$.
This applies with $a=c^2/8>0$ and $a=2h^2>0$ to the first and
third expressions. For $b>0$, the logarithm of
$2\,4^n e^{-bn^2}$ is $\log2+n\log4-bn^2$.
For $n\geq2\log4/b$, it is at most $\log2-bn^2/2$, which
tends to $-\infty$. Apply this with $b=h^4/8>0$ and
$b=2h^4>0$ to the second and fourth expressions. Thus each
logarithm is eventually strictly less than $\log(1/4)$, and
monotonicity of the exponential gives the four required strict bounds.
Choose integer cutoffs for these four eventual statements. Their
maximum, enlarged to include $M$, $1$, and $\lceil1/h\rceil$, is
$n_0$. For every $n\geq n_0$, multiplication of $n\geq1/h$ by
$h>0$ gives $hn\geq1$, and all four eventual bounds remain valid.
Every choice depends only on $c,h,M$.
\end{proof}
