\begin{lemma}[Residual host lower super-regularity]
Let $B,A$ be a finite blow-up instance with candidacy system and let $f$
be a partial compatible embedding on $X$. Let $B_r$ be an instance on
the literal complement subtypes with induced source and host graphs and
the unchanged reduced graph, whose host map is the original host map
restricted to the complement and whose host fibers have size $m$.
Suppose $B$ has host lower-$(\varepsilon,d)$-super-regularity, at most $L$
vertices are removed from each host fiber, and, for every $i$,
\[
 \varepsilon|V_i|\leq\varepsilon'm,\qquad
 d'-\varepsilon'\leq d-\varepsilon,\qquad
 (d'-\varepsilon')m\leq(d-\varepsilon)|V_i|-L.
\]
Then $B_r$ has host lower-$(\varepsilon',d')$-super-regularity.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix an edge $ij$ of the unchanged reduced graph. By
\noderef{InstanceLowerSuperRegular} its original host pair is
lower-super-regular. Write $U=V_i$, $W=V_j$ and
$D_U=f(X)\cap V_i$, $D_W=f(X)\cap V_j$.
These deletion sets are subsets of the corresponding parts.
The subtype projection identifies each residual fiber bijectively with
the ambient part minus its deletion set, so both residual parts have
cardinality $m$.

First verify the lower-regularity premise of
\noderef{LowerSuperRegularDeletionSlicing}. For subsets
$P\subseteq U\setminus D_U$, $Q\subseteq W\setminus D_W$ with
$|P|>\varepsilon'm$ and $|Q|>\varepsilon'm$, the threshold assumptions
give $|P|>\varepsilon|U|$ and $|Q|>\varepsilon|W|$.
The first clause of \noderef{LowerSuperRegularPair} and
\noderef{LowerRegularPair} therefore give density at least
$d-\varepsilon\geq d'-\varepsilon'$ in the original graph.
The assumed degree inequalities and $|D_U|,|D_W|\leq L$ give
\[
 (d'-\varepsilon')m\leq(d-\varepsilon)|U|-|D_U|,
 \qquad (d'-\varepsilon')m\leq(d-\varepsilon)|W|-|D_W|,
\]
by antitonicity of truncated subtraction in the deleted amount.
All hypotheses of \noderef{LowerSuperRegularDeletionSlicing} are now
verified; it supplies lower-super-regularity of the ambient residual pair.

To transfer it to the literal subtype graph, use the subtype value map
from $S=V(G)\setminus f(X)$ into $V(G)$. This map is injective, and
adjacency in $G[S]$ is exactly adjacency of the images in $G$.
The exact fiber-image equalities established above identify the images
of the two residual fibers with $U\setminus D_U$ and $W\setminus D_W$.
The reverse implication of \noderef{LowerSuperRegularPairEmbedding}
therefore transfers the ambient lower-super-regularity to the subtype
pair. Doing this for every edge of $R$ proves
\noderef{InstanceLowerSuperRegular} for $B_r$.
\end{proof}
