\begin{definition}
Let $W_1,W_2,W_3$ be finite vertex sets, let $F_{13}$ and $F_{23}$ be graphs on the corresponding pairs, and let $\sigma:W_1\to W_2$ be an arbitrary map.  The $\sigma$-induced graph $A_\sigma$ is the bipartite graph on the disjoint-union copy $W_1\sqcup W_3$ in which the copy of $v_1\in W_1$ is adjacent to the copy of $v_3\in W_3$ exactly when $v_1v_3\in E(F_{13})$ and $\sigma(v_1)v_3\in E(F_{23})$.  The paper, and the node \noderef{NearEqualFourGraphs}, specialize this construction to the case in which $\sigma$ is a perfect matching in $F_{12}$.  In that case it is the paper's spanning subgraph of $F_{13}$, represented here on a disjoint union so that its two bipartition classes are definitionally disjoint.
\end{definition}
